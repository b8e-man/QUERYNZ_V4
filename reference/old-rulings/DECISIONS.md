# Decisions — the append-only ruling register

> HARVESTED: nothing-durable — this file IS a durable home, not a source. Rulings
> land here and are applied OUT of here into the docs they govern; the harvest
> rule exists to stop knowledge dying in a record, and this record is where it
> goes to live.

> KIND: record (append-only, permanent). Every ruling Arthur makes,
> with the date, the call, one line of reasoning, and WHERE IT LANDED.
>
> WHY THIS EXISTS: rulings get applied into the docs they govern —
> which is correct (one home per fact) — but that scatters them, and
> a workstream doc holding the reasoning becomes a closed record when
> the workstream ends. This is the index that outlives them. It is
> not the home of any decision; it is the map to the homes.
>
> RULE: append the day a ruling is made. Never rewrite an entry —
> correct it with a later one. Architectural rulings ALSO get a fuller
> entry (with what was rejected) in ARCHITECTURE_LOG.md.
