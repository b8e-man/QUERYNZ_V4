# Twelve printed celebrant tables are unread
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: gazette
opened: 2026-10-20

Twelve annual lists (2000-gn1341, 2004-go1177, one a year from
2005-go1336 to 2013-go1631, 2014-go1272) print `Name of Celebrant Name of
Organisation` as a table: `Surname, Given Names Organisation` with nothing
between the given names and the organisation. Each is set aside whole
(catch-log stage `celebrants`). By their commas they hold about 6,000
records each, the largest unread block in the class. The old estate's C226
said the organisation vocabulary that would cut them is derivable from the
delimited lists: those now give 22,661 organisation fields, about 1,400
distinct. A tranche: cut at the longest known organisation that ends the
record, abstain where none or two fit, and measure against the next year's
delimited list for the same people.
