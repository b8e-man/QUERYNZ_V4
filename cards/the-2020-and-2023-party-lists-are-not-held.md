# The 2020 and 2023 party lists are not held
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: house
opened: 2026-10-20

For 2026 the lane reads every candidacy (530, equal to the Commission's
release). For 2020 and 2023 it holds only the results pages, which name
the electorate candidates (2020: 581 from 70 of 72 pages; 2023: 495) and
the members returned; list-only candidates and every list place are
missing. The Commission's 2026 release states the totals they would
reach: 567 candidates in 2023 and 667 in 2020. The source is the
Commission's party list documents for those elections; no old folder
holds them. Nothing is fetched before the switch.
