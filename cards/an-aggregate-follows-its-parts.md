# An aggregate follows its parts
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: comparison
opened: 2026-10-20

A window total, a race total, a size band, a receiver total, a donor's
headline total or the home page's top-forty ranking differs because one of
the figures it sums or orders differs on the same page for a cause of its
own. The comparison assigns such a figure to this card rather than count
the cause twice, and only when every differing part on the page has a
cause. Today: 938 figures (donor 538, pickers 249, home 67, size-bands 61,
party pages 23).

Which side is right: whichever the parts' cards say. This card never
closes by itself; it empties as the others do. Owner: comparison.

Comparison-2 (2026-10-20): where an aggregate's parts are named by its own
path, only those parts count: a race's totals follow that race's
candidates, an electorate's window total its years, the home page's
electorate totals its electorates, a party's funding its card, candidate
donations and public money, the donor categories every party card. The
page's other figures still stand in for a party card's window, a donor's
receivers and a ranking. Today 1,890 (donor 599, pickers 284, electorate
351, home 151, size-bands 30, subject 71).

site-3 (2026-10-20), main's code against this branch's on the same tables:
1,890 → 1,920. The 58 twinned donor pages folded into one carry every row,
so each merged page's totals follow more differing parts; nothing of the
aggregate's own moved.
