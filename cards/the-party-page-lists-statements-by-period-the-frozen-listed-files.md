# The party page lists statements by period; the frozen listed files
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: comparison-2
opened: 2026-10-20

The frozen party page listed one line per financial-statement file, so a
statement held as two documents (Labour's year to 31 December 2024, two
files on the Commission's site) made two lines. The rebuilt page lists one
line per balance date with its documents under it, and lists every
statement the party-finance lane holds, which for five parties is one more
than the frozen page had (Animal Justice, Legalise Cannabis, Conservative,
NZ Outdoors & Freedom, Vision NZ: the 2026 batch). Seven party pages
differ in their count today.

Which side is right: the rebuilt one; a document is its bytes and a
statement is one filing. Owner: party-finance; nothing to fix.
