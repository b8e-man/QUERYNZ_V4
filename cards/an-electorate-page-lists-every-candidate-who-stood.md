# An electorate page lists every candidate who stood
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: comparison
opened: 2026-10-20

The frozen electorate page lists the candidates in the Commission's
candidate-return summary (those who filed a return); the rebuilt page
lists every candidate in the official result. So a race's candidate count
differs (Auckland Central 2020: 10 against 11; 2020 overall 601 against
581 because the Mana result is not held), the rebuilt site has 329 person
pages the frozen site had none for (candidates who filed nothing, and the
2026 list), and 170 candidacies appear on a matched person page only on
the rebuilt side.

Which side is right: neither is wrong; they list different things under
one heading. The page should say which list it shows. Owner: site.
