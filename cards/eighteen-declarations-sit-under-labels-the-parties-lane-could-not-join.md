The parties page counts the Commission's large-donation declarations whose
party label joins no registration: 15 rows on the frozen site, 18 on the
rebuilt one, under seven labels (Advance NZ, DemocracyNZ, Freedoms New
Zealand, New Zealand People's Party, NewZeal, ONE Party, Sustainable New
Zealand Party). Four of those labels are registered parties under another
form of the name, so the lane's join reaches three fewer declarations than
the frozen fold did, and their money is counted here and not on the
party's page. One figure today; the block's total follows it.

Which side is right: the frozen one, where the label is a registered
party's name in another form. Owner: parties (the same join as
`three-party-labels-resolve-to-no-registration`, for declarations).
