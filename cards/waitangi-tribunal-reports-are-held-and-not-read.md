# The Waitangi Tribunal's reports are held and not read
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 2
session: courts-1
opened: 2026-10-21

205 Tribunal documents are held (208 listed, 3 files gone) and recognised
by their profile, `waitangi-tribunal-report`, but not read: they are
reports on claims, not judgments between parties (no neutral citation, no
BETWEEN/AND; claims by Wai number and claimants; decimal paragraphs), and 80
have image pages in their first six pages (the page reader, after ruling 62).
A reading of claims (Wai number, claimants, inquiry district, date, the
document by hash and page) is its own design; until then the legal
counterparty template says "not held" for the Tribunal. The three gone files
are on forms.justice.govt.nz, outside this lane's position.
