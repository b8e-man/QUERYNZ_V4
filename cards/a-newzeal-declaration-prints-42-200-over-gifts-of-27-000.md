`form_check` differs on `ec-listing/2024-07-17/76b5df1420ab` (NewZeal,
gift 4 September 2023, received by the Commission 17 July 2024, not
amended; scan `4c2843a5db0c…`, `Anthony_Tallott_17_July_2024.pdf`): the
table says $27,000.00; the form's total box, read from the scan's own
typed text layer, says $42,200.

The box and the grid beneath it were looked at (crops of those two boxes
alone): the box is typed "$42,200"; the grid itemises two gifts, $18,000
on 3 September 2023 and $9,000 on 4 September 2023, which sum to the
table's $27,000. So the table follows the itemised gifts, and the box
holds a figure the gifts do not make. Whether $42,200 counts earlier gifts
from the same donor, or is a slip, is the Commission's to say; it is never
inferred here. The row keeps the table's figures.

Found by this tranche's text-layer reading: the page reading dropped the
box. To settle it: the Commission's word (Arthur's), or an amended
declaration if one is published.
