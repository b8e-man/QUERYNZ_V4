# Four councils' terms bar automated access, so their candidates' returns are not held
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: Follow the Money, local tier (step 6.3)
session: local-elections-1
opened: 2026-10-22

Auckland, Christchurch, South Waikato and Waimakariri forbid bots, scrapers
and spiders in their website terms (hashes in the lane's position). The lane
does not seed them. Wellington City answered 403. So the local tier is
missing the two largest cities: Christchurch listed 117 returns for 2025;
Auckland's were not found at all. The way in is a request under s 112F(2)(c),
Arthur's: queue `whether-to-ask-the-councils-whose-terms-bar-automated-access`.
Fate at the checkpoint: a known-limits line unless the councils answer.
