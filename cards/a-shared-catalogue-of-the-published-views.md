# A shared catalogue of the published views
kind: idea
state: open
use-case: 9
session: scan-1
opened: 2026-10-21

The subject report (`products/report/catalogue.py`) and the register scan
(`products/scan/views.py`) each keep a catalogue of what every published
view is: how a row is titled, which columns are names and when they are
suppressed, which columns date it, which cite it. A product may not import
another product's, so the second is a copy of the part it needs, and each
new lane's view must be added to both (the board keeps a third, with the
licences). One home that any product may read, written from the lanes'
`pane.md`, would end the copying: `support/` (which products may import),
or each lane publishing its pane as data. For the integrator's weekly look,
not for now.
