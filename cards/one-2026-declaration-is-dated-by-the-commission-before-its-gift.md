Commission's table says it received the declaration on 30 June 2026 and
that the party received the gift on 8 July 2026. Its link is
`Alajamber_Limited_30_July_2026.pdf`, and the scan's own "Date received"
box reads 08/07/2026. Of the 520 declarations held it is the only one whose
