# The frozen site shows the 2017 race, the rebuilt site the 2026 one
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: comparison
opened: 2026-10-20

The frozen electorate pages carry the 2017, 2020 and 2023 elections; the
rebuilt ones 2020, 2023 and 2026 (ruling 361: a race is every year since
the election before it, and the 2026 race is running). So the nine 2017
electorates (Clutha-Southland, Dunedin North, Helensville and so on), the
2017 race on every page, the 2017 year file and every 2017 and earlier
candidacy on a person page are on the frozen side only, and the 2026 races
and the 2025 by-election file on the rebuilt side only. Today: 803
electorate figures and 389 person-page candidacies, plus 222 frozen person
pages whose candidacies all predate 2020.

Which side is right: neither; the template's window is the ruling's. The
frozen person pages from before 2020 come back when the lane publishes
2017 (the house lane holds it; the template does not show it). Owner: site.
