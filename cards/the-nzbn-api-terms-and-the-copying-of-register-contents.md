# The NZBN API's accepted terms are not held, and copying register contents is the lawyer's question
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: -
session: societies
opened: 2026-10-20

The societies lane copies officers from the NZBN API with Arthur's key. The
API page states no licence and no rate limit and points to
`api.business.govt.nz/copyright` and `/privacy`; the Companies Office's
copyright page licenses its material under CC BY 4.0 but excludes "the
public notices and contents of the registers". Two things are open:

1. The terms text accepted when the key was issued is not held. Arthur (or
   a session with his word) reads the portal's terms under his account and
   keeps a dated copy beside `docs/legal/positions/nzbn-register-entities.md`.
2. Whether storing and showing register contents is permitted copying is
   for the lawyer's review of the positions (track G), before anything that
   shows society officers is sold. Capture goes on meanwhile (ruling 61).
