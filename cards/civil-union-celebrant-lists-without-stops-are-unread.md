# Civil-union celebrant lists without stops are unread
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: gazette
opened: 2026-10-20

Three annual civil-union lists (2013-go8151, 2014-go7521, and 2015-go7367,
which also marks its names M and CU) and four smaller notices print `Surname, Given, address, Town` with no
stop between records, so where one record's town ends and the next
surname begins cannot be told from the text. Each is set aside whole ("an
address list with no stops"). The addresses would be private in any case;
only the names would be published. A reading would need the list's
alphabetical order and a town list. Low value: the same people are in the
delimited weekly lists of those years.
