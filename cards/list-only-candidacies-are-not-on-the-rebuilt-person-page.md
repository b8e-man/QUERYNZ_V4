# List-only candidacies are not on the rebuilt person page
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: comparison
opened: 2026-10-20

The frozen person page lists a candidacy on the party list alone, with no
electorate, and its list place ("official result: list seat"). The rebuilt
page lists electorate candidacies only, though the house lane holds list
places. So a list-only year is on the frozen side only (67 candidacies,
Adrian Rurawhe 2023 among them) and 228 frozen person pages whose
candidacies are all list-only or before 2020 have no rebuilt page at all.

Which side is right: the frozen one; the list candidacy is a fact of the
result. Clears when the person page shows list candidacies. Owner: site.

Site-2 (2026-10-20): the person page shows list candidacies: a 2020 or 2023 list seat from the results (route list, the list place "not held"
because the house lane does not hold those lists) and the 2026 list from the Commission's publication with its place. Measured against the frozen
2026-10-20T0620, rebuilt in scratch: 67 list candidacies fell to 53, and the 228 frozen pages whose candidacies are all list-only or before 2020
stayed. Still needs: the 2020 and 2023 party lists in the house lane (house card `the-2020-and-2023-party-lists-are-not-held`) for the 53
list-only candidates who were not elected, and Arthur's ruling on the window for the pages before 2020. Owner: house for the lists, then site.
