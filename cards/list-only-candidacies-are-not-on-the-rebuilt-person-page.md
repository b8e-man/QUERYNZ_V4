# List-only candidacies are not on the rebuilt person page
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: comparison
opened: 2026-10-20

The frozen person page lists a candidacy on the party list alone, with no
electorate, and its list place ("official result: list seat"). The rebuilt
page lists electorate candidacies only, though the house lane holds list
places. So a list-only year is on the frozen side only (67 candidacies,
Adrian Rurawhe 2023 among them) and 228 frozen person pages whose
candidacies are all list-only or before 2020 have no rebuilt page at all.

Which side is right: the frozen one; the list candidacy is a fact of the
result. Clears when the person page shows list candidacies. Owner: site.
