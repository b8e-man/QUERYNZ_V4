# The old estate's interests reading filed minor-party members' declarations under the member before
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: interests-expenses
opened: 2026-10-20

The old `mp_interests.parse` recognised a member's header only when the
party was one of eight words, and only with no bracket in the name. So
Māori Party, United Future, Progressive and Mana members, independents
printed "Name (Electorate)", and one member with a nickname in brackets
were not members to it: their declarations joined the return of the member
printed before them. Its own register (`mp_interests.registers` in
`nzbn_canonical`, read through the standby) counts 2 to 8 fewer members
than this lane in 19 of 21 editions (2006: 113 against 121); every
difference measured is one of those headers.

The frozen product reads that register. If it shows any MP's declared
interests, some carry another member's. For the comparison with the
frozen site (step 4.5): expect these differences, and they are the old
estate's. No change to the old estate (hard limit 19); Arthur's call
whether it is a published figure that is wrong.
Blocks: nothing.
