# Councils' own lists of contracts awarded are not held: their terms bar commercial copying or ask for a request
kind: finding
state: accepted
use-case: 9 government procurement integrity; 10 tender and award intelligence (coverage of councils)
session: procurement-1
opened: 2026-10-22

Step 6.3's third source, surveyed 22 October 2026 (`registers/council_procurement/survey.md`, 45 councils). The
award notices on GETS already cover 76 councils (6,545 current notices). Off GETS:

- Clutha lists 17 awards with supplier and price; its terms bar copying "for any commercial purpose".
- Tauranga lists its current tenders on its own site and none of its awards are in the GETS file; its terms allow
  non-commercial use only.
- Dunedin publishes its contract awards quarterly as PDFs (2021 to September 2026), usable "upon request".
- Queenstown Lakes' "Awarded Council Contracts" page sent a Radware validation page; TenderLink (Hutt City, Upper
  Hutt, New Plymouth) a login page or an Incapsula challenge: failed reads, not asked again.
- VendorPanel (Waimakariri) shows public tenders to registered suppliers only.

Nothing captured. `registers/council_procurement` is a monthly watch of the four pages whose words decide it, red
the day one changes. What closes it: a council's leave (queue `whether-to-ask-councils-for-their-award-lists`,
Dunedin first), or the councils tier ruled off the products (step 6.4), which makes this a known-limits line.
