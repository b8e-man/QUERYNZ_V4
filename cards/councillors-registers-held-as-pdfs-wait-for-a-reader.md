# Most councillors' registers are PDFs, held and not read until ruling 79 lifts
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: none (step 6.3)
session: councils-1
opened: 2026-10-22

Of 78 councils, one (Invercargill) publishes its register as a structured page the lane reads; most publish a PDF
summary, seven one file per member (Christchurch, Hastings, Mackenzie, Matamata-Piako, Tauranga, Wairoa, Auckland).
The capture lands them whole by hash (`councillor_interests.documents`); nothing reads them (ruling 79: no document
reading until both boxes are up). When it lifts: a profile per PDF shape first (profiles before readers), and the
reader withholds real property, homes, addresses, contacts and spouses' interests exactly as `read.py` does. The
survey's search flagged Waipā's and Wairoa's as printing property where members live. Also behind blocks (held):
Dunedin's, Wellington City's and Waikato Regional's HTML registers. Six councils publish none the search found.
