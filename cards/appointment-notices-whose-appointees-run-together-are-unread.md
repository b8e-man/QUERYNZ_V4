# Appointment notices whose appointees run together are unread
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: gazette
opened: 2026-10-20

Of 4,509 notices titled "Appointment…" in the government sections, 1,995
give no appointee and 463 appointees inside read notices are catch-log
lines. Two causes seen in masked shapes: lists whose names the capture ran
together with no separator ("Ann Example Bob Sample as members"), and
offices filled by role alone ("appoints the chief executive of …"). The
second is no appointee at all; the first needs a name cut (the house lane's
members, or an alphabetical order) and is a guess without one. The check
holds what is read: 125 of 134 "Honourable" appointees since 2021 are MPs.
