# Donor pages start at the window
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: comparison
opened: 2026-10-20

The frozen donor page lists a donor's whole history back to 1996 and sums
it; the frozen party page lists Part A rows for every year held. The
rebuilt pages start at the template's window (2017; the queue asks whether
2019). So a year before 2017 is on the frozen side only: 67 donor years
and 538 party-page row years today (the Alliance's 1996 rows among them).

Which side is right: neither; the window is the template's (ruling 347 for
Follow the Money's own window). Owner: site.
