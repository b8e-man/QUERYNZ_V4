# The two readings of the interests register count positions and properties differently
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: comparison-2
opened: 2026-10-20

A member's positions and properties come from the Register of Pecuniary
and Other Specified Interests on both sides, under two readings. The
frozen site's reading gave one line per anchored body with aliases folded
and company status joined; the lane's gives one line per declaration
across the editions in the window, each cited to page and line. The
counts differ on 175 of the matched member pages today (positions on 175
in one direction or the other, properties on 159; a page can differ on
both). The frozen reading is known to have misfiled some declarations
(interests-expenses' card on minor-party members); the rebuilt reading has
not been read against the register page by page.

Which side is right: neither is shown wrong; the member's own return
decides, and the rebuilt line cites the page to open. Owner:
interests-expenses, with a sample read against the register.
