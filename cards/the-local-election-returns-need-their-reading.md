# The local election returns need their reading (after node1, ruling 79)
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: Follow the Money, local tier (step 6.3)
session: local-elections-1
opened: 2026-10-22

`registers/local_elections` lists every return its councils link and lands
each file by hash, unread: no amount and no donor is on any view. The reading
is its own card once both boxes are up (ruling 79): the Schedule 2 form's
totals and Part A donors (over $1,500), as `registers/candidate_returns` reads
the parliamentary form, with a page reader for the scans and a splitter for
the combined files (one per ward or election at Waitaki, Marlborough, Gore,
Horowhenua and others). The form prints the candidate's and each donor's
address: a private twin only (hard limit 15).
