# A candidate's given name differs between the return summary and the results
kind: finding
state: closed
use-case: 1
session: comparison
opened: 2026-10-20
closed: 2026-10-20

Napier 2020: the frozen page, from the Commission's candidate-return
summary, lists Ian Gaskin ($782.50 of expenses); the rebuilt page, from
the official results, lists Camden Gaskin. Same surname, same race, one
candidate. Found where a candidate figure one side lacks has a same-surname
candidate on the other side of the race.

Which side is right: the rebuilt one for who stood (the results are the
record); the summary's given name is for the candidate-returns lane to
join on when it publishes. Owner: house, then candidate-returns.

Closed 2026-10-20 by house-2. The rebuilt side is right, and the house lane
has nothing to change: the Napier 2020 result page prints "GASKIN, Camden",
and the lane keeps the name as filed. The same cause covers more than this
one figure: 11 figures and 7 whole pages that the comparison files under
`candidacies-the-frozen-site-shows-and-the-house-lane-lacks`
(Dave/David KENNEDY, Jack/Jacobus GIELEN, Tubby/Christopher HANSEN,
Aladdin/Ala' AL-BUSTANJI, Chris/Christopher PERLEY, Geoff/Geoffrey MILLS).
candidate-returns joins a return to its candidate on year, electorate and
surname, so the summary's given name does not stop that join.
