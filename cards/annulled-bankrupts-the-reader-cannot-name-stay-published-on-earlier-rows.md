# Annulled bankrupts the reader cannot name stay published on earlier rows
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: gazette
opened: 2026-10-20

A person an annulment names is withheld from `pub.gazette_bankruptcies` on
that row and every earlier row with the same surname and given names (552
rows). 46 annulment notices name their person in a form the reader does not
take, so that person's earlier adjudication row, where one was read, is still
published by name. Options: read the remaining forms, or withhold every
adjudication row from the year before any unread annulment (over-withholds).
The annulment's legal effect for publication is a question for the
bankruptcy product's legal page (hard limit 9) before any product shows
these rows.
