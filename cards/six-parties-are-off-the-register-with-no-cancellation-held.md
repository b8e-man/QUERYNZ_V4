# Six parties are off the register with no cancellation notice held
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: parties
opened: 2026-10-19

The notices export registers six parties that the register page of 19
October 2026 does not list and that no held notice cancels: Outdoor
Recreation NZ (2002), Jim Anderton's Progressive (2002, renamed twice),
Destiny New Zealand (2003), New Zealand Family Rights Protection Party
(2005), New Zealand People's Party (2017) and Heartland New Zealand
(2020). The old estate's Gazette capture is missing their cancellation
notices; it holds 136 notices from 24 February 2000, one for all of
2008. They publish as "not on the register page; no cancellation held",
`off_register_by` the page's day, with no `cancelled_on`. Which years
such a party owed a return is therefore not known from the documents
held. The unlock is the cancellation notices, which the Gazette lane's
capture would bring (no fetch from this lane before the switch).
