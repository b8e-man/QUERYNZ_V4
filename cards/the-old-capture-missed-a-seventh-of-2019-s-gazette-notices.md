# The old capture missed a seventh of 2019's Gazette notices
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: gazette
opened: 2026-10-20

By the Gazette's own numbering (one sequence a year across every
section), the old estate holds 5,153 of 2019's 5,967 notices (86.4%), its
first held number being 50; every other closed year from 2000 is 95.7% to
99.3%. 2019 is inside the Follow the Money window. `check.py` prints each
year's figures, and the missing numbers can be named one by one from the
gaps. Filling them is a capture, which waits for the switch (no capture
job before it) and the Gazette's legal position (hard limits 4 and 9).
