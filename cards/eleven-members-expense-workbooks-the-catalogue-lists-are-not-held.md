# Eleven members' expense workbooks the catalogue lists are not held
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: interests-expenses
opened: 2026-10-20

The government data catalogue's package `member-of-parliament-expenses`
(`package_show.json`, held) lists 42 workbooks; the old `mp-expenses` job
holds 31. The eleven it does not hold are the ones the catalogue links on
www.parliament.nz/media/… rather than on the catalogue itself: every
quarter of 2015 and 2016 except October to December 2016, April to June
and July to September 2017, October to December 2019 and January to March
2020 (the last two inside the Follow the Money window). Each is a line in
`expenses.set_aside` (`stage = 'listing'`) with its URL.

Not fetched (step 4.1: nothing is fetched before the switch). The old notes
say www.parliament.nz shows a challenge to automated reads; hard limit 6
means a challenged read is a failed read. A route is Arthur's: a hand
download into the old folder (as he did for the interests editions), or
the same files elsewhere on the catalogue or www3.parliament.nz. The lane
reads them on its next load with no change.

The catalogue also lists PDF and HTML releases; see the card
`expense-releases-published-only-as-pdf-are-not-held`.
