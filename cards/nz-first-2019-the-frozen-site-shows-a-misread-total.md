# NZ First 2019: the frozen site shows a misread total
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: party-returns
opened: 2026-10-20

The lane now publishes New Zealand First's 2019 return
(`ea8b4bbbe195416eab10c81d5aafc7fb3b9aef611a4eb1a54c9aa6adda661fdc`) with a
declared total of $369,535.17; the frozen site shows $368,535.17. **The
rebuilt side is right.** The declaration's parts are Part A $52,325.00 and
Part G $317,210.17, the rest nil; each is printed again on its own part
page (`TOTAL FOR PART A $52,325.00`, `TOTAL FOR PART G $317,210.17`), and
Part A's eleven rows add to $52,325.00. The parts make $369,535.17, which
is the Commission's own by-year figure. The printed total is a degraded
scan whose third digit both page readers took as an `8`; the form adds
its own totals, so the filer's arithmetic is not the doubt.

The lane takes the parts' sum only when every part page agrees
(`read.recover`, `total_check = parts-confirmed`). The comparison will
show this one party-year differ (home, party page, size bands) under this
card. Nothing to fix on the rebuilt side; on the frozen side it is a
published figure off by $1,000, which is an emergency fix only on
Arthur's word (hard limit 19). Default: no fix to the frozen product.
