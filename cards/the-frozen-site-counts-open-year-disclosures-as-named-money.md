kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: comparison
opened: 2026-10-20

party's named window, its over-the-line window and the 2026 race (ACT's
2026 race: $4,442,307.73 against the rebuilt $2,070,272.07; the gap is
2026's $2,372,035.66 of declarations). The rebuilt site shows them as
declarations beside the returns and never sums them in (ruling 193). The
declared figures by year agree. Today: 153 figures on the home page and
the party pages.
