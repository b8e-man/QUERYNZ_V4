# The frozen site drops Part A rows its own total includes
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 1
session: comparison
opened: 2026-10-20

On a party page the frozen site lists fewer Part A rows for a year than
its own named total for that year sums. ACT 2019: one row of $160,000
against a named total of $188,000; the rebuilt page has the second row
($28,000, Gibbs via J B Gibbs Trust) and its rows add to $188,000. Eleven
party-years today, each found the same way: the rebuilt rows equal the
rebuilt named figure, the frozen rows do not equal the frozen one.

Which side is right: the rebuilt one. Not fixed in the frozen product
(hard limit 19). Owner: comparison.
