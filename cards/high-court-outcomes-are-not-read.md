# The High Court's outcomes are not read
kind: finding
state: open
use-case: 2
session: courts-1
opened: 2026-10-21

The courts lane reads the outcome of 74 of the 83 Supreme Court and Court of
Appeal judgments held, from the lettered orders under JUDGMENT OF THE COURT,
as a closed vocabulary. The High Court puts its result in its last
paragraphs or in prose, and sentencing notes end in a sentence: 0 of 65 read.
A deterministic "Result" section reader would take some; the rest is the
model arm the old estate sanctioned behind a review (allegations recited are
not findings). The legal counterparty template shows the High Court's
judgments without an outcome until then.
