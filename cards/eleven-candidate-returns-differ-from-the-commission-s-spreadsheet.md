# Eleven current candidate returns differ from the Commission's spreadsheet
kind: finding
state: closed
use-case: 1
session: candidate-returns
opened: 2026-10-20
closed: 2026-10-21

Measured on 21 October (candidate-returns-2) on all 1,105 returns, each
difference set against three things: the Commission's second statement
(its listing page's own figures for the same candidate), the return's own
schedule pages (every `TOTAL FOR PART X` it prints), and, where a scan was
in doubt, the declaration page itself, looked at. With the 60 scans read on
20 October the list grew to 15; 13 remain.

**Ours is the filing; the spreadsheet carries other figures (5).** 2023
amendments filed February 2024: Rongotai `0442e03561ce`, Northcote
`07138cf64fb6`, Taieri `0e01e189ba2c`, Ikaroa-Rāwhiti `1446c6720a81`, Port
Waikato `2fed6f4e8502`. The listing, which links the amended return, prints
our figures to the cent; only the spreadsheet (updated 24 September 2024)
differs. It likely still carries the original filing's figures.

**The Commission's typing (5).** Its listing and spreadsheet agree with
each other; the declaration and the return's own schedule totals agree
with ours: Auckland Central 2020 `405025782059` (19,380.54 against .34),
Nelson `7614409b6449` (5,969.84 against 5,969.48), Tāmaki Makaurau
`7b6d40587064` (8,961.42 against .41), Nelson `a2d51b57fdc2` (773.18
against 773.08), Rotorua `fc7020f5f8a6` (35.85 against 38.85).

**The Commission's own figure, with nothing on the return to support it
(2).** Auckland Central 2023 `7cc1ef28b9d9` (expenses 30,056.83 against
32,402.05) and Bay of Plenty 2020 `ba6477b09414` (3,131.66 against
3,685.92): Parts F and G on their own pages add to ours. The Commission
may have added something after review; the return does not show it.

**The return contradicts itself (1).** Northland 2020 `1348a1ec2972`: the
declaration page, looked at, declares Part A 13,750 and total 13,750; the
Part A page's printed total (page reader) is 5,400, the Commission's
figure. Its Part A is set aside (the rows do not add to 13,750).

**Ours was wrong; the Commission was right (2), fixed.** Rotorua 2020
`ca579c46524e` (the page shows Part G $470, total $470) and Tauranga 2020
`98161ad95f6d` (the page shows NIL and $0.00; we read Part A 500): both
from a scanner's own text layer, which dropped or misread handwritten
boxes. A declaration on a picture page now stands only on its printed
total; both are set aside for a page reading (`replay rereads`).

The headline rule stands: the Commission's figure, linked to its file,
with ours beside it (`pub.candidate_return_totals` carries both).
