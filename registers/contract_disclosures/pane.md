# Agency contract disclosures, on the board

One pane, "ACC health contract costs" (`pub.agency_contract_costs`): ACC's own Official Information Act answer of
injury claims active and their costs through each of its 158 health contract classes, by financial year (2013 to
2022), accident region and territorial authority. It names no supplier; it sizes what ACC spends through each class
of its contracts, where. Suppressed cells show as ACC printed them ("<4", "<$100"). The pane's one figure: the
printed totals the rows were held to (837 compared, 0 wrong on 22 October 2026; class-level claims cannot be summed, because every authority-year suppresses one). Agencies' awards are on the award
notices pane (`pub.award_notices`); no agency surveyed publishes its own list of awards (`survey.md`).
