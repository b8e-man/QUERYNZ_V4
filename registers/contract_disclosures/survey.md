# Where agencies publish the contracts they award: the survey of 22 October 2026 (procurement-1)

Through the fetch gate, 19:55 to 20:05 NZDT, serial, 5 s apart (the catalogue's 10 s honoured). The gate's ledger
holds every request.

## The collected file first

The Government Procurement Rules send every agency's contract award notice to GETS, and the ministry publishes
them as one open-data file: the award notices the tenders lane already holds (29,874 notices, 278 posting agencies
in the current era). The government data catalogue was searched for any other collected file (`contracts awarded`,
`procurement contracts`, `procurement`, `contract`, `awarded`, `tender OR tenders OR suppliers`): none. Two
agencies publish a file of their own contracts there:

| Package | Agency | What | Licence | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `health-contract-data` | ACC | "Health contract data", one XLSX, catalogue date 18 August 2023 | CC BY 3.0 NZ | captured and read |
| `msd-and-myd-contracted-funding-f20` | MSD | active contracts with providers of employment, disability, youth and social services, year to 30 June 2020, one XLSX | none stated | not landed |
| `social-services-contract-mapping` | MSD | a link to the retired contractmapping.govt.nz | "other" | not asked |

## The agencies' own sites, by spend

24 agencies: each home page, then its procurement page where one was linked.

| B. Procurement page sends the reader to GETS | Health NZ, Education, NZ Defence Force (and its SmartProcure login), MFAT | Policies, purchase-order terms and codes of conduct; no list of awards |
| C. No list among the procurement page's links | Ministry of Defence, Conservation | |

No agency surveyed publishes its own list of contracts awarded as a structured page or file. The award notices
on GETS are the record; ACC's file adds a register of one agency's contracts with its providers.
