# registers.contract_disclosures

Step 6.3: agency contract disclosures (use cases 9 and 10). Surveyed 22 October 2026 by procurement-1 (`survey.md`):
the one collected file of awards is the ministry's GETS award notices, already held by the tenders lane; no agency
of 24 surveyed by spend publishes its own list of awards as a structured page or file (nine refused or challenged
us; the rest send readers to GETS). The government data catalogue holds two agencies' files about their contracts:
ACC's "Health contract data" (CC BY 3.0 NZ), captured, read and checked against its own printed totals; MSD's
contracted funding for 2019/20, with no licence stated, not landed.
