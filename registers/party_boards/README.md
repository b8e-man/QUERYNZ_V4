# registers/party_boards — the boards the parties declare

A lane reading the held party-website capture (the frozen site's
`party-boards` capture, 1,061 files, read from the document store by hash,
never fetched) into one row per declared board role: party, the name as
declared, the role as declared, the page and its capture date as the
citation, source tier `declared`. Ruling 72 (21 October 2026): built, not
deployed: the tables are published under `pub`, nothing goes on the Follow
the Money site and nothing enters the links until Arthur rules.

`check.py` and `tests/` are its deliverable.
