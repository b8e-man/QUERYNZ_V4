# registers/limited_partnerships — the limited partnerships register

Step 5.3 (for use case 13). The Companies Office's register under the
Limited Partnerships Act 2008: one row per partnership (number, NZBN, name,
status, dates, registered office, which is shown) and one row per general
partner as the register states it, dated. Limited partners are not public
and are not sought. Asked politely through the fetch gate with our own key,
one entity at a time, as the companies monitor asks.

Built by `sanctions-1`, with `registers/sanctions/` and
`registers/building_practitioners/`.
