# MPs' declared interests, on the board

One pane per member and edition: the header as printed (name, party,
electorate), then each category by its heading's name, the declarations
beneath it verbatim, with the edition and page as the citation. The
category is the heading, never the number (the register renumbered in
2018; the number shows as printed, small). Real-property declarations show
as "real property, withheld": their text is private (hard limit 15) until
Arthur rules otherwise. A member whose return printed "No interests
declared" or "Return not received by due date" shows that line. Across
editions a member's panes line up by name only once the person links of
step 4.2 exist; this lane does not join them.
