owns: schema `parliament` (`written_questions`: one per record of the MCP's `parliament-written-questions` corpus, the Parliament API's fields as columns under their own names, the question and reply text whole; `api_items`: one per record of `parliament-bills`, `parliament-bills-historical`, `parliament-sc-submissions`, `parliament-sc-reports` and `parliament-sops`, keyed `<corpus>/<id>`, typed by corpus and `documentType`, every field of the API's document shape a column, the capture block whole; `wayback_pages`: one per record of `parliament-wayback`, an archived parliament.nz page's fields and its text whole; each row read from the version the pull landed last, `served` false where the MCP no longer serves the record; `files`: every document, read or an older version or set aside; `set_aside`, the catch log, with the pull's own catch lines at stage `pull`, and every archived page that is the site's refusal ("Unauthorized Request Blocked", known by its title and its short fixed text: a challenged read is a failed read, hard limit 6) as `blocked: the archived copy is the site's refusal (hard limit 6)`, the document kept in the store; `address_flags`: every published text whose shape names an address (`shapes.py`: street, legal, numbered), by table, key and column; `street_scan`: the load's whole scan of the published views by the check's second pattern, per view and text column the rows scanned, their load stamp and the count found); `readings`: the text reader's one reading per written question and archived page (model_read.py: pieces, names, the one summary line, truncated, the request's batch, the served model, reader, profile and version); `people`: every person and body the text reader found a document naming (key `<sha>/person/<n>`, name as printed, kind person/company/agency/body/other, role member/minister/official/witness/submitter/other, role_text, of_organisation, the document's own table, key and day, place "text reader, piece p, name n"), each text column with `<column>_names_address` beside it; a document without a reading is waiting on `qv4-parliament-read`, one whose text the profile cannot split (a WAV file served as a page) or whose reading is unusable is in `set_aside` at stage `read`, named; `files` status `older text` marks an archived page's older snapshot whose text differs from every later one (a document of its own for the reader; none today)); `parliament_private` holds nothing
reads: the documents of source `query-nz` that `support/mcpsource` landed from the ingest MCP's seven parliament corpora, by hash, chosen from `mcpsource.arrivals` by `landed_at` (rulings 78, 94, 96; profiles `mcp-parliament-written-question` and `mcp-parliament-wayback-page` at version 2 (`profile-proposed.jsonl`: the text reader's budget of 24,000 characters and the split rule, sizes measured on the live tables 31 October 2026), `mcp-parliament-api-item` v1); the stored readings of the text reader (model_read.py: Flash-Next, profile `text-reader`, temperature 0, through `support.modelclient`, one reading per document hash stamped with the reader, the served model and the prompt's hash, replayed by every load; ruling 50); position `position/parliament.md` (for docs/legal/positions/). Fetches nothing: the provider captured parliament.nz off-estate, through a challenge the estate may not pass (hard limit 6); the fetch gate holds the host. The bills, committee papers and SOPs are metadata records whose files the provider has not captured: nothing to read, no reader
publishes: `pub.parliament_written_questions` (number, year, Parliament, Parliament's document id, title, `member_id`, the minister, released day, whether replied, the question and the reply whole), `pub.parliament_api_items` (corpus, document type, title, bill, stage, committee, member, party, dates, the capture block, `text` whole), `pub.parliament_wayback_pages` (the page's address, the archive's copy and stamp, title, type, words, the text whole); each row with its document hash and the page's own address where the record gives one (`citation_url`: sc-submissions and wayback pages; NULL for written questions, bills, reports and SOPs, whose records carry none); nothing withheld (rulings 101, 102): beside each text published whole, `<column>_names_address`, true where its shape names an address, set at the load, by which each product applies hard limit 15 at its own surface; `pub.parliament_people` (ruling 123: the links lane reads it; the board catalogues it in the merge after): key, name, kind, role, role_text, of_organisation, each text with its flag, source_table, source_key, dated, citation_url, document, place, reader, model, profile, profile_version; never a link, never a claim
outside check: `check.py`, per corpus: the MCP's `corpora` count against the pull's cursor (at least 99%), arrivals plus catch lines equal the cursor, records plus older versions plus set aside equal the documents, the served records plus the pages set aside as blocked equal the places taken, `measure`'s n_docs against the cursor and its corpus_words against the rows' `text_words` (within 1%); written questions: every served question's number and year unique within its Parliament, and at least 99% replied (683,517 of 683,524 measured, 27 October 2026: all but 7); over the views, each flag's count stated and equal to `address_flags`, and no text column (found by introspection) holding a street address, by a second pattern in Postgres, where its flag is false or absent: run over every row at the load and stamped (`street_scan`), the check holding the stamp to the view's rows and load and scanning afresh a fixed sample of 5,000 rows per view (36 s on the live tables, 28 October 2026; the board gives 120 s); the blocked pages counted on the line; the text reader per source table: documents, read (pieces, answers cut at the token cap), names read by kind and role, waiting on `qv4-parliament-read`, set aside at read, each stated, and a readings or people row under another profile version than the load expects fails; the papers stated as waiting on the provider's capture; any catch line, other set-aside line (stage read excepted) or unread field fails; exits 1
jobs: proposed, the integrator queues: `qv4-parliament-load` (processor, 2 GB, nightly 04:30 NZDT after `qv4-mcp-pull` (02:30) and `qv4-beehive-load` (04:00), `python -m registers.parliament.load`, deadline 60 min, query time limit 600 s); the merge gate's staging step loads it the same way (`load.py`); `qv4-parliament-read` (model-profile `text-reader`, 0.5 GB, class whenever, pieces, `can_share`, concurrency 4: `python -m registers.parliament.model_read --piece <sha256>`; the keys from `--pieces`: every archived page waiting, newest first, then the written questions' batch leaders (one piece reads its batch of up to 16 questions of one year and number range in one request); the lane's next load picks the readings up; about 21,980 page pieces (35,400 requests) and 42,700 question pieces the first time)
