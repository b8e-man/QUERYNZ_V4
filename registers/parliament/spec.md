owns: schema `parliament` (`written_questions`: one per record of the MCP's `parliament-written-questions` corpus, the Parliament API's fields as columns under their own names, the question and reply text whole; `api_items`: one per record of `parliament-bills`, `parliament-bills-historical`, `parliament-sc-submissions`, `parliament-sc-reports` and `parliament-sops`, keyed `<corpus>/<id>`, typed by corpus and `documentType`, every field of the API's document shape a column, the capture block whole; `wayback_pages`: one per record of `parliament-wayback`, an archived parliament.nz page's fields; each row read from the version the pull landed last, `served` false where the MCP no longer serves the record; `files`: every document, read or an older version or set aside; `set_aside`, the catch log, with the pull's own catch lines at stage `pull`) and `parliament_private` (`wayback_texts`: each archived page's text; never published)
reads: the documents of source `query-nz` that `support/mcpsource` landed from the ingest MCP's seven parliament corpora, by hash, chosen from `mcpsource.arrivals` by `landed_at` (rulings 78, 94, 96; profiles `mcp-parliament-written-question`, `mcp-parliament-api-item`, `mcp-parliament-wayback-page`, v1); position `position/parliament.md` (for docs/legal/positions/). Fetches nothing: the provider captured parliament.nz off-estate, through a challenge the estate may not pass (hard limit 6); the fetch gate holds the host. No model reads anything
publishes: `pub.parliament_written_questions` (number, year, Parliament, Parliament's document id, title, `member_id`, the minister, released day, whether replied), `pub.parliament_api_items` (corpus, document type, title, bill, stage, committee, member, party, dates, the capture block), `pub.parliament_wayback_pages` (the page's address, the archive's copy and stamp, title, type, words); each row with its document hash and the page's own address where the record gives one (`citation_url`: sc-submissions and wayback pages; NULL for written questions, bills, reports and SOPs, whose records carry none); never a question, reply or page text
outside check: `check.py`, per corpus: the MCP's `corpora` count against the pull's cursor (at least 99%), arrivals plus catch lines equal the cursor, records plus older versions plus set aside equal the documents, the served records equal the places taken, `measure`'s n_docs against the cursor and its corpus_words against the rows' `text_words` (within 1%); written questions: every served question's number and year unique within its Parliament, and at least 99% replied (683,517 of 683,524 measured, 27 October 2026: all but 7); any catch line, set-aside line or unread field fails; exits 1
