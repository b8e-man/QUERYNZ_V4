# registers/overseas_investment — overseas investment decision summaries

Step 5.3, a held-and-unread source (for use case 12, property developer
due diligence). The decision summaries the Overseas Investment Office
publishes for each consent it grants or declines, and each transaction
notified under a standing consent (applicant, vendor, the land or
business, consideration where stated, the decision and its date), from
2017 (ruling 71), captured verbatim by hash through the fetch gate under
the four questions, read by profile into one row per decision and one per
named party, checked against the office's own listing, year by year.
Street addresses of land are withheld from the views and held private
(hard limit 15); a name the office withholds is withheld everywhere (16).

Built by `oio-1`; `spec.md`, `capture.py`, `read.py`, `load.py`,
`publish.sql`, `check.py`, `sample.list`, `tests/`,
`profile-proposed.jsonl` and `position/` are its deliverable.
