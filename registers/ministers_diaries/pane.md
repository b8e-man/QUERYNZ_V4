# Ministers' diaries, on the board

One pane per release (`pub.minister_diary_releases`): the minister as the
release prints them, the month, the file name, and the PDF's address as the
citation (where the provider served only a file name, the pane says "file
name only: no address served" and makes none up), with the document's hash
small beneath it. Then its counts: entries, entries with a withholding,
locations held back. Then its meetings in date and time order
(`pub.minister_diary_entries`): the day, the time as released, the
portfolio, the meeting, who attended and the location, each as released.
A field the release withholds shows its own words ("Withheld under
s9(2)(f)(iv)") in the note style, never blank. A location held back shows
"location held back: names a home" or "…: a street address with no place
of business named"; a street address masked in the meeting or attendees
shows as "[street address held back]". Names are not links: nothing here is
joined to a person's row (product (b), after links). Every pane notes that
the estate did not fetch the PDF: the provider read it off-estate (ruling
78). The provider's `text` is in no view.
