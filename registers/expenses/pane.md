# MPs' and ministers' expenses, on the board

One pane per member or minister and period: each column's figure as printed
(accommodation, travel, the total; money outside the total, such as the
Inter-Parliamentary Office's or Cabinet-approved international travel, shown
beside it and never added in), the release as the citation (document, sheet,
cell), and the publisher's own grand total for the period beside the sum of
the lane's lines, with any column that differs named. A line whose categories
do not add to its own total is not shown; it is in the catch log. Names are
as printed; the party is the release's own heading, NULL where it states none.
The same person and quarter can come from two releases (Internal Affairs
reprinted three 2018-19 quarters in one workbook); each row cites its own.
