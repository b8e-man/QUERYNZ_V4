owns: schema `gazette` (`notices`: every notice held, one per Gazette notice number, typed by its section code and recognised format; `celebrants`: one per celebrant a Registrar-General's list names; `prohibitions`: one per person a notice prohibits from managing companies; `liquidations`: one per company a liquidator's appointment names; `liquidators`: one per liquidator it names; `files`; `set_aside`, the catch log) and `gazette_private` (`celebrant_addresses`: an independent celebrant's address as printed; `prohibition_addresses`: a prohibited person's `Of:` address; never published)
reads: the once-only export of every Gazette notice the old estate holds as text (`incoming_news` articles at gazette.govt.nz/notice/id/ with `nzbn_canonical.gazette.notices`), made 20 October 2026 by `capture.py --export`, landed by hash and pinned (sha256 47de7e09…, 208,461 lines, 207,133 notices); profiles `gazette-notices-export`, `gazette-celebrant-list`, `gazette-director-prohibition`, `gazette-liquidator-appointment`; `keys.dates`; the companies lane's `pub.companies`, to join each liquidated company to its entity. Fetches nothing; the export is never run at load
publishes: `pub.gazette_notices` (every notice: number, section, family, format, status, title, date, address; never the text) and `pub.gazette_celebrants` (surname, given names, organisation, the list's action and statute, the period) and `pub.gazette_prohibitions` (the person and aliases, the action, statute, term, start and end, what revoked or varied it; the name withheld where the notice was revoked), `pub.gazette_liquidations` (company, number where printed, who appointed, statute, date and time, the register's entity and how it was joined) and `pub.gazette_liquidators` (name as signed, role, whether the body names them too; no address, telephone or email is read), each row with its citation. Public appointments, bankruptcies and the other insolvencies are typed in `pub.gazette_notices` and not yet read
outside check: the Gazette's own counts, printed in its notices (`check.py`): the year's highest notice number against the numbers held, and each celebrant series' highest "Notice No." against the notices held and read; every notice the Gazette titles "Notice Prohibiting Person" read as a prohibition, and every notice a later one revokes or varies by number held, read and marked; every notice filed under section `al` taken by the liquidator profile; the liquidated companies against the Companies Office register (`pub.companies`): at least 97% of company rows joined, by printed number or by the one name alive on the date, and at least 98% of held printed numbers agreeing by name; a company the register does not hold is a catch-log line; a held numbered celebrant notice or titled prohibition not read, an unmarked revocation, an `al` notice left unread, a register share below its floor or never joined, or a closed year with no notice, fails and exits 1
jobs: none of its own; the load runs in the merge gate's staging step (`qv4-gazette-load` is its run record, about 12 s for every notice). No capture job before the switch
