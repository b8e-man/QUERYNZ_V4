owns: schema `gazette` (`notices`: every notice held, one per Gazette notice number, typed by its section code and recognised format; `celebrants`: one per celebrant a Registrar-General's list names; `prohibitions`: one per person a notice prohibits from managing companies; `liquidations`: one per company a liquidator's appointment names; `liquidators`: one per liquidator it names; `company_notices`: one per company a removal notice (`ds`), a winding-up application (`aw`) or a receivership (`ar`) names; `receivers`: one per receiver a receivership names; `bankruptcies`: one per person a bankruptcy notice names; `files`; `set_aside`, the catch log) and `gazette_private` (`celebrant_addresses`: an independent celebrant's address as printed; `prohibition_addresses`: a prohibited person's `Of:` address; `bankruptcy_addresses`: a bankrupt's address and formerly-of address; never published)
reads: the once-only export of every Gazette notice the old estate holds as text (`incoming_news` articles at gazette.govt.nz/notice/id/ with `nzbn_canonical.gazette.notices`), made 20 October 2026 by `capture.py --export`, landed by hash and pinned (sha256 47de7e09…, 208,461 lines, 207,133 notices); profiles `gazette-notices-export`, `gazette-celebrant-list`, `gazette-director-prohibition`, `gazette-liquidator-appointment`, `gazette-removal-notice`, `gazette-winding-up-application`, `gazette-receivership`, `gazette-bankruptcy-list`, `gazette-no-asset-procedure-list`, `gazette-bankruptcy-notice`; `keys.dates`; the companies lane's `pub.companies`, to join each liquidated company to its entity. Fetches nothing; the export is never run at load
publishes: `pub.gazette_notices` (every notice: number, section, family, format, status, title, date, address; never the text) and `pub.gazette_celebrants` (surname, given names, organisation, the list's action and statute, the period) and `pub.gazette_prohibitions` (the person and aliases, the action, statute, term, start and end, what revoked or varied it; the name withheld where the notice was revoked), `pub.gazette_liquidations` (company, number where printed, who appointed, statute, date and time, the register's entity and how it was joined) and `pub.gazette_liquidators` (name as signed, role, whether the body names them too; no address, telephone or email is read), `pub.gazette_company_notices` (kind, action, company, number, section, ground, who gave notice, the plaintiff's name and never its address, the notice's date that matters, court and reference, the register's entity and how it was joined) and `pub.gazette_receivers`, `pub.gazette_bankruptcies` (the person, alias, occupation, kind and day; never an address; a person an annulment names withheld on that row and every earlier row naming them), each row with its citation. Public appointments and administrations are typed in `pub.gazette_notices` and not yet read
outside check: the Gazette's own counts, printed in its notices (`check.py`): the year's highest notice number against the numbers held, and each celebrant series' highest "Notice No." against the notices held and read; every notice the Gazette titles "Notice Prohibiting Person" read as a prohibition, and every notice a later one revokes or varies by number held, read and marked; every notice filed under section `al`, `ds`, `aw`, `ar` or `ba` taken by its profile; the companies against the Companies Office register (`pub.companies`), by printed number or by the one name alive on the notice's date: at least 97% of liquidation rows joined and 98% of their held printed numbers agreeing by name, at least 95% of removal and of winding-up rows joined, 88% of receivership rows (a company sold out of receivership is often renamed); a company the register does not hold is a catch-log line; a held numbered celebrant notice or titled prohibition not read, an unmarked revocation, an `al`, `ds`, `aw`, `ar` or `ba` notice left unread, a person an annulment names published by name, a register share below its floor or never joined, or a closed year with no notice, fails and exits 1
