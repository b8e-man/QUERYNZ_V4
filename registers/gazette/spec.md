owns: schema `gazette` (`notices`: every notice held, one per Gazette notice number, typed by its section code and recognised format; `celebrants`: one per celebrant a Registrar-General's list names; `prohibitions`: one per person a notice prohibits from managing companies; `files`; `set_aside`, the catch log) and `gazette_private` (`celebrant_addresses`: an independent celebrant's address as printed; `prohibition_addresses`: a prohibited person's `Of:` address; never published)
reads: the once-only export of every Gazette notice the old estate holds as text (`incoming_news` articles at gazette.govt.nz/notice/id/ with `nzbn_canonical.gazette.notices`), made 20 October 2026 by `capture.py --export`, landed by hash and pinned (sha256 47de7e09…, 208,461 lines, 207,133 notices); profiles `gazette-notices-export`, `gazette-celebrant-list`, `gazette-director-prohibition`; `keys.dates`. Fetches nothing; the export is never run at load
publishes: `pub.gazette_notices` (every notice: number, section, family, format, status, title, date, address; never the text) and `pub.gazette_celebrants` (surname, given names, organisation, the list's action and statute, the period) and `pub.gazette_prohibitions` (the person and aliases, the action, statute, term, start and end, what revoked or varied it; the name withheld where the notice was revoked), each row with its citation. Public appointments, bankruptcies and the other insolvencies are typed in `pub.gazette_notices` and not yet read
outside check: the Gazette's own counts, printed in its notices (`check.py`): the year's highest notice number against the numbers held, and each celebrant series' highest "Notice No." against the notices held and read; every notice the Gazette titles "Notice Prohibiting Person" read as a prohibition, and every notice a later one revokes or varies by number held, read and marked; a held numbered celebrant notice or titled prohibition not read, an unmarked revocation, or a closed year with no notice, fails and exits 1
jobs: none of its own; the load runs in the merge gate's staging step (`qv4-gazette-load` is its run record, about 12 s for every notice). No capture job before the switch
