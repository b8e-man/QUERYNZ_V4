# Template 08: recruitment board screen

Step 5.2, first in the plan's order; runs on held data and waits on nothing.
A subject report (the shape in `products/report/`) for a candidate for a
board or senior role: the person's directorships and shareholdings with
their dates, insolvency and prohibition notices from the Gazette, charity
and society roles, political money given or received, declared interests
where the person is or was a member, each cited; namesakes listed apart.
Opened on main for the session that builds it; it reads only the pub views
its `template.toml` names. Nothing here is a product until its checkpoint.
