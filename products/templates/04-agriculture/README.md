# Template 04: agriculture counterparty

Step 5.2, second in the plan's order; runs on held data and waits on
nothing. A subject report (the shape in `products/report/`) on a company or
a person a farming business is about to trade with: the entity and its
officers with dates, holdings of record, Gazette receiverships,
liquidations, winding-up applications and removals, bankruptcies and
prohibitions of the people, charity and society roles, political money,
each cited; namesakes listed apart; a removal from the register is not
insolvency. Opened on main for the session that builds it; it reads only
the pub views its `template.toml` names. Nothing here is a product until
its checkpoint.
