# Template 11: healthcare provider and practitioner checks

Step 5.2, in the plan's order after templates 5 and 6. A subject report
(the shape in `products/report/`) for a health provider's credentialing or
procurement lead, run on the companies register's roles, offices and
states, the Gazette's insolvency notices, prohibitions and bankruptcies,
the societies and charities registers, with the practitioner registers
(Medical Council, Nursing Council and the other responsible authorities)
named as not held until a lane publishes them.

Built by `healthcare-1`; the contents of this folder are its deliverable
(`template.toml`, `rules.py`, `limits.md`, `checkpoint.list`, `tests/`).
