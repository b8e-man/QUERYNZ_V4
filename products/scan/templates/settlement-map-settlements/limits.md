# settlement-map-settlements: known limits and the checkpoint

Template 3, the settlement each body's directory entry names. The six
scans, the shared limits and the ten bodies read by eye are on
`../settlement-map-iwi/limits.md`. For the register scan only, never for
the public site.

## Known limits

- **The directory's own words.** The Act, deed or collective the entry
  names, the stage as the lane reads the flag (69 "settled: Act", 8 "deed
  signed", 7 "as printed", 10 collective, 16 "mandate recognised"). A
  mandate is a stage, never a settlement, and is shown as that stage.
- **The flag and its text can differ.** Te Korowai o Wainuiārua carries a
  mandate flag whose text says a deed was signed on 29 July 2023; the line
  shows the stage and the text as printed side by side.
- **A settlement key is the settlement's, not the row's.** The Central North
  Island collective is one key across 8 bodies, two Te Tau Ihu Acts across
  3 and 4. Each body's line now cites the page its row was read from (the
  shape numbers a citation per row; before, 13 lines cited another body's
  page and the check passed: card `a-settlement-key-names-several-rows`).
- **One key, two bodies:** Ngāti Hāua Iwi Trust carries the Waikato Ngāti
  Hauā Iwi Trust's 2014 Act (card `the-iwi-directory-key-folds-two-bodies-into-one`).
- **Te Haeata is not held** (login; hard limit 6), so no commitment,
  obligation or date from the settlement commitments database is on a line.
