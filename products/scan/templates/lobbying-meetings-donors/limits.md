# lobbying-meetings-donors: known limits and the checkpoint

Step 8.5 (use case 15, lobbying; step 6.2's template 4, part (b)). Each meeting resolved to the companies register by the
company's whole name, and a party-return donor filed under the company's name beside it (near). 45 lines on 2 November 2026; the links'
organisation-name rule joins 30 party-return donors to companies, none of them a company a diary names by its whole name.

## Known limits

The template's `limits` are the full list. In short: ministers only; a meeting joins a company only where the whole
attendees text is the company's name (lobbying-attendees-donors starts from each attendee instead); every line is near; the donor is the Commission's; nothing says why a meeting was
held or that a donation and a meeting have anything to do with each other.
