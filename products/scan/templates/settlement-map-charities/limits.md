# settlement-map-charities: known limits and the checkpoint

Template 3, the charity of the body's name and each officer the Charities
Register lists on it. The six scans, the shared limits and the ten bodies
read by eye are on `../settlement-map-iwi/limits.md`.

## For the register scan only, never for the public site

Officers are named people (hard limit 14). A suppressed name shows "name
suppressed", the row kept (none in this run).

## Known limits

- **By name alone, near.** The directory prints no charity number; the
  charity's registered name is folded and compared. A charity's other
  names are not compared. A removed charity of the name meets and says so.
- **Namesakes marked.** 228 lines are one of two or more charities of
  the same folded name, each its own line.
- **Every officer the feed holds**, past ones with their end date: of a
  body's officers most are past (Te Rūnanga o Toa Rangatira, 49 of 56).
  The line carries role, status and kind (a person or a body).
