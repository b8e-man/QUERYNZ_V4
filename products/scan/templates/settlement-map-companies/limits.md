# settlement-map-companies: known limits and the checkpoint

director on it. The six scans, the shared limits and the ten bodies read

## Known limits

- **The business number register whole.** Of the entities met, most are
  not companies: charitable trust boards, incorporated societies, public
  bodies; the entity type is on the line. A society here is listed again
  in `settlement-map-societies`; the two are never merged.
- **Directors only where the file lists them.** A trust board, a society
  or a public body has none in the bulk file, so its director step reads
  "none found in": a blank, not an empty board. 4 of 67 entities list one.
- **By name alone, near; namesakes marked** (7 lines: Te Rūnanga o Ngāi
  Tahu meets two LTDs and a public body). A removed LTD of the name meets
  (Ngāti Kahungunu ki Wairarapa Limited, removed).
- **A corporate trustee under another name meets nothing:** "… Trustee
  Limited" keeps "Trustee" through the fold.
