# products/scan — the register scan shape

Step 6.1. A scan asks one question of every row of a register or a join of
registers ("which suppliers share a director with a donor?") and lists the
answers with their dates, their numbered citations and their grade
(confirmed, near, contested), under the two trust settings and their
defaults. It re-runs when a register it read has loaded since. Nothing from
step 6 goes on the public site; the surface listens on the local network.

Built by `scan-1`; `spec.md`, the runner, the template contract, the two
proving scans, `check.py` and `tests/` are its deliverable. The four scan
templates of 6.2 are later sessions.
