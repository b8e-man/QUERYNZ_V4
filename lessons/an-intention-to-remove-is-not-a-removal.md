A Gazette "intention to remove" is not a removal: count it as a company's end only when the register's status says the company ended, or a lapsed notice from 2008 reads as a failure on a company that is registered today.

The property template's serial pattern counted every removal notice on a
principal's company as a failure dated from its publication, as the
construction template does. On the checkpoint, BROOKS HOMES LIMITED had
intentions to remove published in 2008 and 2011 and is Registered in the
August 2026 file; two of the twelve subjects' first results (a director
with six "removed" companies, all still registered; a young GP company
whose own intention had lapsed) rested on such notices. A company that
files, or draws an objection, stays on the register, and the notice is all
that is left. The fix: a removal counts only where the company's status is
ended; a pending one (objections not yet due) is amber on the entity, a
lapsed one info with the status beside it. Generally: a notice of an
intention is not the event; join it to the register's present state
before counting it.
