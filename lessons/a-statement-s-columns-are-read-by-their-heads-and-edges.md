A party's financial statement is read column by column from its own page: the reported year is the later column head (not always the first), a `$000` head scales every figure, and a dash is a nil column where the page's figure columns end and a minus anywhere else.

Animal Justice 2024 prints `2023  2024`, so its first column is the prior
year, and both columns pass income − expenditure = surplus: the arithmetic
cannot tell the years apart, only the heads can. Labour's audited
statements are in thousands to one decimal (`2,667.7`), so the tolerance
is the printed precision, not a dollar. `-            339` (NewZeal 2025)
is minus 339 while `-           12,143` (TOP 2024) is nil then 12,143:
the gap is the same, the column ends are not. A two-digit figure in the
figure column is a figure (NewZeal's `83`), and one left of every amount
is a note number.
