The construction template's phoenix signature by place compared a failed
company's registered office with a new company's. The fixtures passed; on
the real tables not one pair ever matched, and a measurement said why:
4,735 of the 4,945 companies in liquidation with an office in the file have
one dated from their liquidation (the liquidator's, usually an address
fifty companies share). The fix: compare only an office dated before the
failure, block otherwise, and card the past offices (the change history).
