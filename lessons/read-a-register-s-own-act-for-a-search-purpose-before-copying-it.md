Before copying a public register, read its own Act for a search-purpose section: a register can be open to anyone and still be searchable only for the purposes its Act names, so a bulk copy is unlawful though the site says "copy freely".

The licensed building practitioners register is Crown copyright that "may be used, copied and re-distributed free
of charge", and its search page has no login. But the Building Act 2004, s 307, allows a search "only for a purpose
referred to in section 299", and s 308 makes any other search for personal information an interference with
privacy under the Privacy Act 2020. Question 4 of the four (what the register's own statute limits) decided it, not
questions 1 to 3. The same session found the opposite trap twice: terms that bar the robot but not the copy (MFAT),
and terms that allow a copy but not a compilation (the UN, Police). Read all four answers before the first fetch of
anything but the terms themselves, and hold the lane behind one `authorised:` line when any answer says no.
