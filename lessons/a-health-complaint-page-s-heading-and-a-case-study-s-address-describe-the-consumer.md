The Health and Disability Commissioner anonymises consumers by letter, but its listing tiles ("Delays in recognition
and treatment of sepsis in a young child") and its case-study addresses (`/delay-in-diagnosis-of-cancer/`) carry
exactly what re-identifies a consumer beside a provider and a date. The decision pages' own addresses are file
numbers and safe. A tile's heading is still useful as a change signal: keep its hash in the fingerprint. The guard
must be tested by planting a detail (an age, a label, a relation, a condition, a place) in a fixture and in a
published view, and by mutants that switch each class off.
