Ruling 109's rule relates two companies of one name, one removed and the
other registered within twelve months, where a person is confirmed on both.
The measure of the gap (12,607 pairs in the window, 2 sharing a person)
looked like a rule that rarely fires. The reason was the instrument: the
companies lane publishes people for a removed company only where a monthly
file it holds still listed it, so Christopher & Banks Limited 3482091,
removed in 2024, has no people at all, and no rule over the published rows
could relate it. Count each end's coverage (here: removed companies with any
person row) at the first measurement, and say whether the case that raised
the rule can be seen, before writing the rule.
