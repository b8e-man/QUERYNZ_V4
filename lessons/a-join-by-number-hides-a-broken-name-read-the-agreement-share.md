When a reading joins the register by a printed number, read the share whose names agree, not the share joined: the society reader joined 99.9% while a strip of office letters was eating "ED" off every "INCORPORATED".
area: Gazette notices, societies, charitable trusts, company names, register joins, outside checks
paid-for-by: 2026-10-20, gazette session 4, step 5.3

The dissolution lists print each body's registration number, so every row
joined `pub.companies` by number whatever its name looked like: 30,692 of
30,720 joined on the first measure. The same measure said 21,514 of those
numbers named a different body, and a sample of ten showed one fault: the
pattern meant to drop the old registry office's letters ("I.S.", "WN")
allowed no space before them, so it took the last two capitals of
INCORPORATED. Requiring a space moved the agreement from 30% to 90% and the
join did not move at all. A join by number is the register's identification
and stays right when the name is wrong; only the agreement share says the
reader is. The same share, sampled, told renames apart from faults in the
restorations (548 rows, companies renamed after they were restored). The
earlier lesson (`join-a-reading-to-the-register-it-names-before-trusting-its-own-counts`)
found its faults through names that did not join; this one had none.
