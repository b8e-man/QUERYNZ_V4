Ruling 114 extended the donor organisation rules from `pub.companies` to
`pub.societies`, `pub.limited_partnerships` and `pub.charities`. The
companies lane copies every Companies Office register, so `pub.companies`
carries each society and partnership again (`register` `other_incorporated`,
same NZBN, number and dates), and `links.org_names` read it all. Joined by
NZBN: 53,271 of 53,271 societies and 3,380 of 3,380 partnerships were
already there, and 31,532 of 46,139 charities are one of those bodies.
Adding the registers naively would have counted each such body twice and
turned the existing confirmations near. Measure the overlap first, fold a
body on two registers into one by its number, and say the true gain (here:
the same 292 confirmations, none new) before the request promises more.
