When rows move off one surface onto another, move every check that read them to the new one: a check left reading the old surface passes on nothing while the new one shows the fault it was written to catch.
area: board, checks, surfaces, panes

Why: board-12 took rows out of the board's results pane (ruling 111); a row is now read only in its entity's dossier. Check item 16 (ruling 92 (2): a director's company reads as its name, never its bare key) looked for `<th>company</th>` cells in the results pane. With no row there it would have found no cell and, after a small edit, passed. Pointing it at the dossier instead found the dossier printing `company: companies/9429…`, bare, on every director's row since board-11 built it, because the dossier wrote its fields through its own `fields_text`, not through `panes.far_cell`.

How to apply: when a change moves what a surface shows, grep the check for every selector and phrase that reads the old surface (`id="results"`, `<th>`, `<strong>view</strong>`) and decide for each whether its subject moved. Point it at the subject's new home and run it before fixing anything: a red there is the real state. Where the old surface must now be empty, add an item that fails if the thing reappears there (here item 26: no row's card in the results pane outside a dossier). Related: "A fold fixed on one surface is not fixed" (old lessons, 6-working-style).
