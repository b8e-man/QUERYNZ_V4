searching the regulator's name with "data" before the first society was asked. The societies' own lists still
matter for what the file lacks (before 2021, the purpose as printed), so the survey ran, but as a class-by-class
card rather than 31 readers. Step 6.3's other two sources (regional development funding, tertiary funding) have
their funders' own collected releases to look for first.
