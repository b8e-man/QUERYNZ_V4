The old `party_lifecycle.renames()` paired a gazette party with a roll
party by registration date because "of 13 amendment events not one
carries a party name". Read again, the 136 notices state 15 changes of
name: "change the party name of X to Y", "by X to change its party name
to Y", and "New Party Name: Y … Previous Party Name: X" (plus a
corrigendum to one of them). The window rule is still worth keeping as the
check that a stated rename and the register page describe one
registration; all four renames it had found agree with the statements.
The cut that broke the old parser is glue, not wording: a name ends at
"…Partyat the party's request" and "Internet PartyThe Internet Party
logo", and an uppercase "A" that begins a new block ("MANA Movement")
must not be taken for one.
