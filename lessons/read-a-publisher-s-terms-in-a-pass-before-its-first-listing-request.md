The survey of the 78 councils asked, per council, the terms page and then the returns pages, in one serial pass.
Auckland, Christchurch, South Waikato and Waimakariri bar bots and scrapers in their terms ("You must not use
automated tools, such as bots or web scrapers"), so their returns pages had been asked once before anyone knew.
The bytes stayed in the scratch folder, unlanded, and nothing more was asked, but the order was wrong. Terms are
the cheaper read and they decide whether the next request happens: ask every publisher's terms first, classify
them, then ask listings only of the ones whose terms allow it. Also found: the terms differ council by council
(CC BY 4.0 at Greater Wellington; personal or non-commercial reuse at most; none found at four), so a statute
that obliges publication does not make a class of source one set of terms.
