A date a provider serves as ISO can still have been read month for day: check a monthly release's dates against the month its file name gives, because the ISO shape hides the error.
area: registers/ministers_diaries, any register of monthly releases read off-estate
paid-for-by: 2026-11-29, integrator, office-holder-1's evidence showed "September 2025.pdf" dated 9 January to 9 December

The provider read nine diary releases' day-first dates month-first: 4 September 2025 came as 2025-04-09, every
entry of the 1st to the 12th became the 9th of some month, and in five releases the 13th to the 31st, which no
month can hold, were not served at all. Our reader checked only that the date was ISO, so 218 entries went into
the register, the links and a review set dated up to eleven months wrong. The test that catches it is cheap: a
release whose file name names one month should have nine in ten of its entries in that month, and a swapped entry
sits on the day that is the month's number. registers.ministers_diaries.read.unswapped now puts those right and
keeps the date as served; the missing days are the provider's to land.
