To learn the shape of a Te Kāhui Māngai iwi page, a skeleton printer listed
each tag with its class and href and cut the cell text short. The text was
masked; the hrefs were not, and one organisation's `mailto:` link printed a
named contact's email address into the session. It went into no file, row,
card or log. The viewers now replace every href with `[masked]` and print a
label's name with its value's length only; the lane reads an allowlist of
labels and its tests assert that no email or phone run on the real pages
reaches a row.
