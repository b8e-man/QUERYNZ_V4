A text page's own layer is a partial key on most Commission form pages (pdftotext prints a field's label and never its typed value; a scanner's OCR layer is itself a reading; a logo's words are an image): test the key before calling a run invented.
area: reading documents, page readers, scoring, EC returns
paid-for-by: 2026-10-19, trial session, step 3.7

The plan's free key (step 3.4) is right in principle and failed on first
use: 122 of 455 text pages "invented" text under the five-word rule, and
on inspection the runs were the form's printed instructions, the typed
values of form fields, an auditor's standard paragraphs, and the
Commission's bilingual name off its logo. A census without reading
(`score.key_quality`: pdfinfo's Form line, pdffonts, pdfimages, a
garbage test) marks 298 of the 455 keys partial. The rule that holds:
judge a run against both of pdftotext's orderings, count it only when
two or more of its longer words are nowhere on the page, treat a run
another family of reader also reads as text the key lacked, and leave a
run on a partial key undecided until another family has read the page.
On complete keys GLM-OCR then fails 4 pages, all of them text the layer
lacks for a reason the census does not yet see (instructions drawn as
paths, most likely); pass two decides them.
