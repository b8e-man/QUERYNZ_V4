The name step first marked every name join with the count of rows there
under the same folded name. From the award notices to the companies
register that is right: two companies called SSC HOLDINGS LIMITED are two
bodies, and the line must say it is one of two. From a company to the
award suppliers it is wrong: "Pattle Delamore Partners" is one supplier
named on 38 notices, and the first read-by-eye showed every one of its
lines as "one of 38 rows ... namesakes". The step now counts only when the
template says the rows there are bodies (`namesakes = true`), and the
template for the other direction leaves it off. Ask what a row of the view
is before counting rows as people or companies.
