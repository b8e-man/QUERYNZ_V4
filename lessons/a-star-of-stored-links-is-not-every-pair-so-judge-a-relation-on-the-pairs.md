`company-name@1` stores two companies under one name apart from a year in
brackets as a star from the smallest bare-named company. For a near
identity that loses nothing: the group walk, or a reader, reaches every
member through the anchor. The successor rule asks something of each pair
(does a person sit on both?), and "A (2018) is succeeded by A" says nothing
about "A (2018)" and another company named A. Measuring over the stored
star gave 928 pairs sharing a person; judging every pair the name pattern
makes gave 17,366 pairs and 951 relations. When a new rule reads an old
rule's links, ask whether the old storage shape answers the new question.
