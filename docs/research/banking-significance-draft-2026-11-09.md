# Banking director history: what is significant (draft, 9 November 2026)

Ruling 146: each product names its significant information. This is the first draft for use case 3, held until the
diagnostic subject report has been run (Arthur, 9 November 2026). Not yet agreed.

The question: every company this person has been involved with, and how each one ended.

## Keep

| Information | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Companies: name, number, status, dates registered and removed | how each company ended |
| Directors and shareholders: role, date appointed | what the person did there |
| Liquidators, receivers, administrators appointed | the failures |
| Gazette: liquidations, company notices, bankruptcies, banned directors | the failures, as published |
| Court judgments where the person or company is a party | disputes and debts |
| FMA and Commerce Commission enforcement | regulator action |
| Registered office, only to flag a shared ("thousand-company") address | spots nominee setups |

## Cut

| Information | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Donations, party returns, candidate returns, election promoters | not significant to credit |
| Societies and charities officers | not significant to credit (could be optional) |

The template today reads 32 views, these included.

## Links it may follow

- Follow: the same company number; the same full name with the same registered office.
- Do not follow: same name, nothing else. Namesakes are counted ("3 others share this name, not shown"), never
  listed.
