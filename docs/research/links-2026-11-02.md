## Method

`links/measure.py` m13 `m13_declared_bodies` and m14 `m14_charity_officers`, with the rest of `links.measure` (`--only 13`).
The pairs are drawn by a fixed hash: the seed "8.2" over the row keys. The organisation key is `keys.names.organisation_key`.
A body is "live on the date" when it was registered on or before the return's date and not removed before it, by the
body's own register's dates in `links.bodies`; a body removed on no stated day is never live. The same name means the
first-and-last key of `pub.names`. A charity is its company or society only by a shared business number (ruling 124).
