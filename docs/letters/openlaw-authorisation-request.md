Subject: Request for authorisation under your API terms (clauses 3.6 and 3.7)

Kia ora,

I run Follow the Money (followthemoney.nz), a New Zealand public-records
service that links registers and cites every statement to its source. I
hold an OpenLaw API key (issued 15 August 2026).

Under clauses 3.6 and 3.7 of your API Terms of Use, may I have your
authorisation to:
1. keep a copy of each case record and build tables from it (parties,

2. show the citation, court, date, parties (with suppression applied),
   cited judgments and statute links in our product, attributed to
   OpenLaw NZ with a link to each case, where parts of the product may
   later be paid for.

We would read the cases one request at a time, three seconds apart,
overnight, and then only new cases nightly. A suppressed or anonymised
party is never named on any surface. Happy to discuss any other
arrangement you would prefer.
