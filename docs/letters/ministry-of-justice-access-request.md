Subject: Automated access to Judicial Decisions Online and published tribunal decisions

Tēnā koutou,

I run Follow the Money (followthemoney.nz), a New Zealand public-records
service that links public registers and cites every statement to its
source. We would like to hold a copy of the Ministry's published court and
tribunal decisions so that a company's or person's appearances before a
court or tribunal can be shown beside the companies, charities and
donations registers, each with a link to the decision on your site.

The decisions are public and we read politely: one request at a time,
five seconds apart, overnight, each document once, with a reader that
identifies itself as "QueryNZ/1.0 (research aggregator; contact:
admin@followthemoney.nz)". But Judicial Decisions Online does not answer
automated requests at all, and the robots file for the decision files
under justice.govt.nz/assets asks automated readers to stay out. We have
respected both and ask rather than work around them.

Could the Ministry provide, or point us to, a permitted route for:

1. the back catalogue of High Court and Court of Appeal judgments on
   Judicial Decisions Online (a bulk file, a feed, or our User-Agent
   allowed at the pace above);
2. the published decisions of the Employment Relations Authority, the
   coronial findings, and the disciplinary tribunals and review bodies
   whose decisions sit under justice.govt.nz/assets (the Lawyers and
   Conveyancers Disciplinary Tribunal, the Real Estate Agents Disciplinary
   Tribunal, the Teachers Disciplinary Tribunal and the Legal Complaints
   Review Officer).

Suppression orders are honoured on every surface: a suppressed or
anonymised party is never named. Any terms you would attach, we will
follow.

Ngā mihi,
Arthur
