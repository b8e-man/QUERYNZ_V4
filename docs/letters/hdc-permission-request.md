Subject: Request for permission under your copyright terms (clause 3.2)

Tēnā koe,

I run Follow the Money (followthemoney.nz), a New Zealand public-records
service that links registers and cites every statement to its source.

Under clause 3.2 of your website's copyright terms, may I have your
written permission to:
1. keep a copy of each decision and case study you have published since
   2017, and build tables from them;
2. show from each decision only the structured facts: the file number,
   date, the provider you name, the finding, the Code rights breached and
   whom the recommendations are addressed to, each sourced to your site
   with its publication date and a link to the decision;
3. include that material in a commercial product, where access is paid for.

We keep no consumer detail, no description of the care, no practitioner's
name and no case study's address, and we never reproduce your logo. We
would read the site one request at a time, five seconds apart, overnight,
and then check the listings weekly.

Ngā mihi,
Arthur
