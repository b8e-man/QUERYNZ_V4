# Whether the licensed building practitioners register may be copied or searched for our products at all (the Building Act limits every search to its own purposes)
default: no default. The register is held: `registers/building_practitioners` asks it nothing and has no reading of it. Building Act 2004, s 307: "A search of the register may be carried out only for a purpose referred to in section 299" (to find out whether a person is licensed and their licence history, to choose a practitioner, to contact one, to know who was disciplined in the last three years). s 308: any other search for personal information "constitutes an action that is an interference with the privacy of an individual under section 69 of the Privacy Act 2020". The register's own search page says the same.
blocks: `pub.building_practitioners` (one row per licensed practitioner, for use cases 6, 7 and 12). The Board's published decisions are not affected: they are captured and published (`pub.building_practitioner_decisions`).
recommendation: do not copy the register. Ask the lawyer (track G) one narrow question: is a check of one named practitioner, made when a product's user is choosing or checking that practitioner, a search "to determine whether a person is a licensed building practitioner" under s 299(a)(i)? If yes, the lane gets an on-request lookup of one practitioner at a time, built like the companies monitor. If no, use cases 6, 7 and 12 say "licence status not held; check lbp.govt.nz".
