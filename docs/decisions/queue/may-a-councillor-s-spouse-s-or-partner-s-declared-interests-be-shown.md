# May a councillor's spouse's or partner's declared interests be shown?
default: No. The category is counted by its heading and nothing under it is read or kept
blocks: Nothing
recommendation: Keep the default. Councils publish a summary that can include a spouse's or partner's employment, companies and trusts; that is a private person, not a public office-holder, and showing it beside the councillor's name names them. The councillor's own categories carry the public interest. One cost: Invercargill prints "Employment – self or spouse" as one category, so five councillors' own employment is withheld with it; a rule that keeps such a row only when the council splits self from spouse would recover it.
raised: 2026-10-22 by councils-1. Invercargill City Council's register (the first structured page read): 12 of 71 category rows are a spouse's or partner's. Built as a setting (`read.PRIVATE_PERSON`), not a rewrite.
