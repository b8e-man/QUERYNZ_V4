# May the Gazette lane read the notices that name a person for their conduct: vehicle confiscation orders and professional disciplinary findings?
default: No: both stay typed and unread ("not read" in pub.gazette_notices), and no person is published from them
blocks: Nothing built. The legal counterparty check (use case 2) and the board screen (8) would show a disciplinary finding if read; no template asks for a confiscation
recommendation: Confiscation orders, no: 6,213 notices under section go ("Order for Confiscation", Sentencing Act 2002 s 128/129) each name a convicted person, the offence and the vehicle; a conviction is a new class of source (hard limit 9: a legal position page first), the Criminal Records (Clean Slate) Act 2004 bears on showing an old one, and no use case in step 5 asks for it. Disciplinary findings, yes after a one-page legal position: 96 notices under gn (the Chartered Accountants' and other tribunals) name a member, the finding and the penalty, which a board screen and a conflict check want; each would get its own profile, the person by name and never an address, like the prohibitions
raised: 2026-10-20 by gazette-4 (step 5.3, the unread sections by use case)
