# Whether every process that writes the live database must carry a mark, or only a session needs one
default: only a session needs one (built by fix-support-db-1, 22 October 2026). `support.db.connect` refuses the owner login on `querynz_v4` to a process inside a Claude session (`CLAUDECODE` in its environment) that has no mark (`QV4_PIECE` or `QV4_RUN_ID` from the scheduler's worker, `QV4_INTEGRATOR=1` from the integrator). Anything that is not a session passes. One constant (`support.db.SESSION_VAR`) makes this a setting.
blocks: nothing. The fence the card asked for holds at the default.
recommendation: keep the default. If Arthur wants the stricter rule, a fix session on `support/installer` writes `Environment=QV4_UNIT=%n` into every unit it installs, and `support/db` then accepts `QV4_UNIT` and drops the session test. The stricter rule also stops a hand run from an ordinary shell outside Claude, which the default does not.
raised: 2026-10-22 by fix-support-db-1

Why not the brief's rule (every caller marked): the nine installed units (`qv4-worker@`, `qv4-board`, `qv4-report`, `qv4-scan`, `qv4-offsite-dump`, `qv4-checks-board`, `qv4-checks-heartbeat`, `qv4-full-tier`, `qv4-site-preview`) carry no mark and several open the owner login on `querynz_v4`, the worker daemon first among them. The worker sets `QV4_PIECE` only for jobs cut into pieces, and every lane's `load --job` and `capture` is unpieced. Refusing every unmarked caller would have stopped the scheduler at its next restart. systemd's own `INVOCATION_ID` cannot be the mark: a session's terminal is a systemd service and carries it too.

Also follows, at either rule: the sessions the plan lets touch live things ("What is live, and who may touch it": the scheduler session, the trial session, track sessions with their own jobs) reach the queue on the owner login (`python -m support.scheduler enqueue|cancel|status|report`, default `querynz_v4`), and are now refused there too. At the default their brief says to export `QV4_INTEGRATOR=1` for those commands; recommendation: give them that line in their brief rather than a third mark, since the grant is the brief's. Status and report are reads and are better asked through `bin/q`.
