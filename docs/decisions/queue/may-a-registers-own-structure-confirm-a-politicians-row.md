# May a register's own structure confirm a politician's row: a House row to its seat, and a declared company's director to the member?
default: Yes, as built: `seat-and-window@1` (an interests return or an expense line joined to the seat whose term covers it, same first and last name, the printed electorate agreeing, one party and no two middle names among the seats that fit; otherwise near) and `declared-directorship@1` (a member's return joined to the one director row of the member's name on a company the member declared, where exactly one company of that name was alive on the return's date and the company has one director of that name; otherwise near) grade confirmed. Both are a setting (the grade in `links/build.py`'s two steps), not a rewrite
blocks: Nothing. The subject report and the Follow the Money person page read `pub.groups`; with the default a seat, its returns, its expenses and its declared directorships are one group
recommendation: Yes. Neither rule is a name match: the House prints an interests return and an expense line about the member who holds the seat, and an electorate has one member at a time; and the design already names "a company that person declared" as the supporting link that lets a politician's name on the director register count.
