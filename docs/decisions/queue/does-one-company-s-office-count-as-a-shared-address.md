# Does one company's registered office count as a shared address for two roles on that same company?
default: Yes, as built: rule 2 (same first and last name at the same street address) reads the registered office of the company each role sits on, and two roles on one company share it by construction
blocks: Nothing. It moves 585,220 of rule 2's 942,014 links and most of the measured error: on the known-two-people pairs (two directors with one name on one company at once) rule 2 confirmed 61 of 200 rare, 31 of 200 middling and 5 of 30 common pairs, every one through the shared company's own office (links/measurements.md)
recommendation: Split the case. A director and a shareholder of one company with one name (573,440 links) are the owner-director pattern and the link is right far more often than not; keep it, labelled "same company". Two director roles on one company with one name standing at once (259 links) are two people by the register's own practice, and rule 2 confirms them wrongly; exclude same-company pairs of roles of one kind from rule 2. Both are one setting in links/rules.py and links/build.py, not a rewrite. The rule itself (ruling 221) is yours and is not changed by the links session
