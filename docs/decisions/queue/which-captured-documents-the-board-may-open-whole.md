# Which captured documents the board may open whole, when they may print a person's home address
default: no default
blocks: Ruling 86 (4)'s "source" link for every source but the House lane and the expenses lane. It covers party and candidate returns, disclosures, the Gazette, the companies files, the tribunals, councils and the rest: 54,873 documents are held, 270 of them open.
recommendation: Keep the rule as built: open a source only when its printed form has no field for a person's address, measured over every document it holds. For the forms that do carry one (a donor's address on a return or a disclosure, a bankrupt's in the Gazette), build a redacted copy before opening any of them. That is a page image with the address boxes covered, made by the lane's reader and kept beside the original. Until then the publisher's own copy is linked beside the row. Hard limit 15 says a home address is never shown or extractable on any surface, and a document served whole cannot hide one. That is why this has no default.
raised: 2026-10-26 by board-4. Held behind one setting, `products/board/catalogue.py` `SERVED_SOURCES`: house (167 documents, one address-shaped line, the Commission's own) and expenses (103, none). Every other held document says "source held, not opened here (hard limit 15)", and `/doc/<sha256>` answers 403 without reading its bytes.
