# How long a Building Practitioners Board decision about a named practitioner is shown in the estate
default: three years from the decision's date, the window the register itself keeps for discipline (Building Act 2004, s 299(a)(iv) and s 301(1)(l)(iii)). Older decisions are captured and held, not shown. One interval in `registers/building_practitioners/publish.sql` changes it.
blocks: nothing; the default applies.
recommendation: the default. The Board keeps older decisions on its own site (1,600 listed), so showing them all is arguable, but the statute's three years is the clearest signal of what is fair to a practitioner under the Privacy Act. A template can still say "older decisions may be on lbp.govt.nz".
