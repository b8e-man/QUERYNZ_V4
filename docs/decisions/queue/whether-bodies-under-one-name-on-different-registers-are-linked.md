# Whether bodies under one name on different registers are linked as namesakes (a charity and its company, a society and its company)
default: No default (ruling 106). Today `company-name@1` joins company rows to company rows under one name (87,889 near, one star per name) and nothing joins a charity, a society or a limited partnership to a company or to each other by name; the only cross-register confirmation is the NZBN (`official-number@1`, 4,392).
blocks: The same-name bodies of the Rank Group sitting (ruling 119): a reader collapsing organisation entities by hand has no namesake list to pick from across registers. Use case 14 (charity officers holding company roles) wants the charity beside its company.
recommendation: A near rule `body-name@1` over `links.bodies`: bodies under one name key on different registers, one star per name as `company-name@1` draws it, grade near with the reason "one name on two registers", both windows and whether alive together, never confirmed (the NZBN confirms). Measured: 141,523 name keys are shared by 304,959 bodies; across registers the pairs are companies and societies 55,812 (22,920 both live), charities and companies 32,080 (20,500), charities and societies 9,336 (5,899), companies and limited partnerships 3,381 (3,379).
