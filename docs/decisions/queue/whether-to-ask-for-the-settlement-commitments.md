# Whether to ask for the Treaty settlement commitments (Te Haeata is behind a login; Te Tari Whakatau's terms bar data collection)
default: no default for getting in: making an account on Te Haeata is getting past a lock (hard limit 6) and accepting its terms, which only Arthur may decide. Meanwhile not held: `registers/settlement_commitments` makes no account, asks for no record, and watches weekly that the wall still stands (red the day it falls). The settlement and Act a governance entity's own Te Kāhui Māngai entry names are held (`pub.iwi_directory_settlements`).
blocks: Template 3's commitments half: which agency owes what to which post-settlement governance entity under which deed or Act (use case 14).
recommendation: Two short letters, Arthur's: (1) to Te Puni Kōkiri, which runs Te Haeata: ask for an export of the commitments (settlement, commitment text, agency responsible, commitment type, governance entity), or read access for an estate account, saying what is kept and that it is shown with a link back; (2) to Te Tari Whakatau: ask permission to collect its "Find a Treaty settlement" pages (one per settlement: the deed date and the documents) at one request every 5 s at night, notwithstanding its terms' bar on "data collection activities" (`0f944146…`). Do not make an account before an answer. Alternative, no letter: the deeds and settlement Acts are public (legislation.govt.nz holds the Acts); a reader of them is a document reading, which waits on ruling 79 and on its own position.
