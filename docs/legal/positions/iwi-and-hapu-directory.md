# The iwi and hapū directory, Te Kāhui Māngai (draft position, ruling 61)

hosts: tkm.govt.nz, catalogue.data.govt.nz

Class: Te Kāhui Māngai, the Directory of Iwi and Māori Organisations that Te Puni Kōkiri (the Ministry of
Māori Development) publishes at `www.tkm.govt.nz`: the iwi recognised by the Crown (117 iwi pages linked from
its 13 region maps on 22 October 2026), each iwi's rohe and local authorities, its hapū and marae table, and
its representative organisations with their statutory roles (post-settlement governance entity with the
settlement Act, mandated iwi organisation, iwi aquaculture organisation, RMA iwi authority); national and urban
Māori organisations; non-tribal marae. Lane: `registers/iwi_directory`. Draft, in the lane folder; the
integrator moves it. Read 22 October 2026 through the fetch gate by settlements-1.

1. **Is the page open?** Yes. Every page asked (home, map, browse, the 13 region pages, 10 iwi pages, one
   local authority page, the other organisations, marae and non-tribal marae pages, glossary, about,
   copyright, disclaimer, privacy) answered 200 with no login, key or challenge, 5 s apart. The site's search
   and contact pages are forms and are not asked. Robots (`efc496fd…`): no crawl delay; it names `/rohe`,
   `/images`, `/search`, `/Bin`, which are maps, pictures and forms the lane does not want.
2. **Is storing it lawful copying?** Two statements, which differ. The site's copyright page (`6c3d13e5…`):
   Crown copyright; "may be reproduced for personal, not-for-profit or in-house use without formal
   permission or charge", acknowledged to "the Te Kāhui Māngai website of Te Puni Kōkiri". The government data
   catalogue's record of the same directory (`3377b9be…`, package
   `directory-of-iwi-and-maori-organisations-te-kahui-mangai`, published by the Ministry of Māori
   Development, modified 23 November 2017) states "Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 New Zealand"
   (`CC-BY-NZ-3.0`), which allows copying and commercial use with attribution. The build (in-house, opened to
   no one, hard limit 13) is within both. Before any product shows it beyond the estate, the lawyer says which
   governs, or Te Puni Kōkiri confirms the catalogue's licence (queue: `whether-te-kahui-mangai-is-cc-by`).
   Attribution on every row and view: "Source: Te Kāhui Māngai, Te Puni Kōkiri (www.tkm.govt.nz), CC BY 3.0
   NZ as catalogued on data.govt.nz".
3. **What terms did we accept?** None by click. The disclaimer (`050687f6…`) bars nothing we do: organisations'
   details are "supplied by representative Māori organisations", checked twice a year, and "should not be
   construed as advice … on which iwi authorities or hapū … should be consulted"; rohe maps are indicative;
   the site is not a record of historical hapū and marae. Every view says the source supplies the details.
4. **What does privacy law limit?** Each organisation's entry names people (chair, chief executive, RMA
   contact) with their phone, mobile, email, and the organisation's postal and physical address; a small
   hapū group's postal address may be a person's home. The page is captured whole; the reading reads an
   allowlist of fields only (legal entity, governance structure, hapū represented, area of interest, local
   authorities, website) and the role flags; no name of a person, phone, email or postal or physical address is
   read, kept or printed (hard limit 15). A marae's location is the marae's own and is kept as printed. The
   privacy statement (`74031896…`) covers visitors only.

Crawl delay: none stated. Floor: 5 s, one request at a time, off-peak (22:00 to 06:00 NZ), each page once a
week; about 240 pages a walk. Author: settlements-1 session (Opus), 22 October 2026.
