# The public sector's own lists of its organisations (position, hard limit 9) — DRAFT, not yet captured

hosts: publicservice.govt.nz, treasury.govt.nz, data.govt.nz

Class: the lists the Crown publishes of its own organisations: Te Kawa Mataaho Public Service Commission's list of the
public service and wider state sector (departments, departmental agencies, Crown entities of each type, Offices of
Parliament, state-owned enterprises), the Treasury's schedules of Crown entities and companies, and the same lists as
data.govt.nz publishes them. Read for each organisation's name, its former names and their dates, its type and its
monitoring department. Arthur, 27 November 2026 (ruling A on the name-forms review): the Crown's bodies, the courts and
tribunals are organisations, and the forms the name pass finds for them ("Waitangi Tribunal", "Oranga Tamariki",
"Ministry of Health") should tie to a register that holds them; no register in the estate does. The capture goes to the
provider (5 November 2026: crawls and downloads are the MCP's); this page is written before it.

1. **Is the page open?** Each list is published openly on a government site, no login, key or cookie. A challenge or a
   refusal is a failed read (hard limit 6; ruling 141).
2. **Is storing it lawful copying?** The lists are the Crown's proactive publication of facts about its own machinery;
   the sites license their content for reuse (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 under NZGOAL), credited to the publisher.
   Ruling 242: a governmental source's proactive release is read whole.
3. **What terms did we accept?** None beyond the sites' published licence; no account.
4. **What does privacy law, or the statute, limit?** The lists name organisations. A chief executive named beside an
   organisation is a public office-holder named in that office by the Crown itself; no home address or private detail is
   carried (hard limit 15).

Floor: the provider's polite floor; a list is fetched once and again when it changes.
