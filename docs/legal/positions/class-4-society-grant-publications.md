# Grants published by class 4 gambling societies on their own sites (draft position, ruling 61)

hosts: airrescueservices.co.nz, agt.nz, bluesky.org.nz, constellationtrust.org.nz, dragon.org.nz, firstlightfoundation.co.nz, fourwindsfoundation.co.nz, grassrootstrustcentral.co.nz, grassrootstrust.co.nz, iltfoundation.org.nz, kiwigaming.org.nz, mainlandfoundation.co.nz, milestonefoundation.co.nz, newhorizoncommunitytrust.co.nz, nzct.org.nz, onefoundation.nz, oxfordsportstrust.org.nz, pelorustrust.net.nz, pubcharitylimited.org.nz, rano.org.nz, redwoodtrust.org.nz, akaranacommunitytrust.co.nz, lionfoundation.nz, nstl.co.nz, ttcfltd.org.nz, trillian.nz, trillian.co.nz, trustaoraki.co.nz, trusthouse.co.nz, wecare.org.nz, youthtownfunding.org.nz

Class: the grants lists each operating class 4 society publishes on its own website, as the Gambling Act
2003 requires (s 110(4): approved and declined grants, at least annually). The 31 societies and their sites
are the Department's own list (granted.govt.nz, 21 October 2026). Lane: `registers/grants`. Surveyed, not
yet read: the lane's rows come from the Department's national file, which carries every society's grants
for 2021 to 2025; the societies' own lists are the source for 2017 to 2020 and for the purpose as printed
(carded by class in `docs/status/grants.md`). Draft, in the lane folder; the integrator moves it.

1. **Is the page open?** Surveyed 21 October 2026 through the gate: each society's home page and at most
   three of its own pages a link calls grants (87 page requests and each host's robots file, 5 s apart).
   Every home page answered with no login or challenge but one listed address, `fourwindsfoundation.co.nz/MCCFCT`, which answered 404
   for both societies that share it. Two societies send their grants pages to hosts this page did not name
   (`trillian.co.nz`, now named; `youthtowntrust.org.nz`, not yet): the gate refused, correctly.
2. **Is storing it lawful copying?** The lists are published because the Act requires it, for the public.
   No licence was looked for in the survey; the one footer read closely (First Light's) carries a plain
   copyright line.
   Copying a list whole to read the facts in it (who, how much, when) and showing those facts with a
   citation is what the lane does; the society's own words (a purpose as written) are quoted, not
   republished as a document. Lawyer to confirm, society by society, before anything is sold; until then a
   society's own list is read only for the facts and the window the national file lacks.
3. **What terms did we accept?** None by click. No society's terms page was read in the survey; the society
   capture (carded) reads each one before its first list, and a society whose terms forbid copies is held
   behind one setting and put in the decision queue marked no default, as openlaw-1 did (none known on 21
   October 2026).
4. **What does privacy law limit?** As the Department's file: a recipient may be a person, and declined
   applications are listed. The one list opened (Air Rescue's, January to March 2019)
   prints the recipient's territorial authority; no address was seen, and none would be read. A recipient is kept as printed and never looked up.

Crawl delay: none stated where read. Floor: 5 s, one request at a time, off-peak; each site walked whole
from its grants page (the whole-site doctrine), each file once. Author: grants-1 session (Opus), 21 October
2026.
