# Grants published by MBIE's regional development unit and the Tertiary Education Commission (draft position, ruling 61)

hosts: www.growregions.govt.nz, growregions.govt.nz, www.tec.govt.nz, tec.govt.nz, www.educationcounts.govt.nz, educationcounts.govt.nz

Class: public funding for regions and tertiary education, the last two grant sources of step 6.3: Kānoa, the
Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment's regional economic development unit (growregions.govt.nz:
the Provincial Growth Fund, the Regional Strategic Partnership Fund, the Regional Infrastructure Fund and
others), and the Tertiary Education Commission's funding of providers (tec.govt.nz), with the Ministry of
Education's statistics site (educationcounts.govt.nz) that points to it. Lane: `registers/grants`. Draft, in
the lane folder; the integrator moves it. Read 22 October 2026 through the fetch gate: seven requests.

1. **Is the page open?** growregions.govt.nz answered its home page, "What we have funded" and its copyright
   page with no login or challenge (`33aa40a3…`, `658ba72c…`, `582e4586…`). tec.govt.nz answered the first
   request with an Incapsula challenge: a failed read (hard limit 6); nothing more was asked of it.
   educationcounts.govt.nz answered its statistics index, its tertiary resourcing page and its copyright page.
2. **Is storing it lawful copying?** Kānoa's site is Crown copyright licensed under Creative Commons
   Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0: "free to copy, distribute, and use the material, for any purpose other than
   commercial purposes. You need to get our permission first if you want to use it commercially." Its project
   list ("All Kānoa managed projects", an xlsx, as of August 2026) is that site's material. The estate is built
   to be sold (ruling 2), so copying under a non-commercial licence is not taken to be covered: the file was not
   landed. Facts in it (who was funded, how much) may not be copyright at all, but that is a lawyer's question;
   until it is answered, or Kānoa gives permission, the file is not read (queue). Education Counts is CC BY 4.0;
   its tertiary pages hold aggregate figures, no grant to a named recipient. TEC's terms were not reached.
3. **What terms did we accept?** None by click. Kānoa's terms are its copyright page; TEC's sit behind the
   challenge.
4. **What does privacy law limit?** Kānoa's recipients are organisations, councils, iwi entities and companies;
   some projects name a business that is one person's. TEC funds providers (institutions), not people. A
   recipient would be kept as printed and never looked up; no address is read.

Crawl delay: none stated where read. Floor: 5 s, one request at a time, off-peak. Outcome: nothing read; Kānoa's
file waits on the licence question, TEC on a read that is not challenged (card
`regional-and-tertiary-funding-not-read`). Author: grants-2 session (Opus), 22 October 2026.
