# Licensed building practitioners: the register and the Board's decisions (draft position, ruling 61)

hosts: lbp.govt.nz, mbie.govt.nz, legislation.govt.nz
authorised: register 2026-11-02 ruling 132 (Arthur: "copy: Building practitioners register"); copied whole, politely, through the register's own public search, no address kept

Class: two publications under part 4 of the Building Act 2004, on two
footings. (a) The Building Practitioners Board's published complaint
decisions, the list on `www.lbp.govt.nz`. (b) The register of licensed
building practitioners, searched at `kete-lbp.mbie.govt.nz`. Lane:
`registers/building_practitioners`. `legislation.govt.nz` is named for the
statute's text only. Read 21 October 2026 by sanctions-1; the register's
part revised 2 November 2026 on ruling 132.

**Finding: both are copied. The decisions (a) are captured; the register (b)
was held on its own statute until Arthur's ruling 132 (2 November 2026,
"copy: Building practitioners register"), and is now copied whole.**

1. **Is the page open?** Yes, both, with no login, lock or challenge. The
   decisions list answered 200 to four pages and three documents on 21
   October 2026, 5 s apart. `www.lbp.govt.nz/robots.txt` disallows only
   `/admin`, `/assets/_generated_pdfs/*`, `/Security/` and error pages, and
   states a crawl delay (5 s) for Bingbot only; `kete-lbp.mbie.govt.nz`
   answers 404 for a robots file. The register's search page was read once
   (the page, not a search); no search of the register has been made.
2. **Is storing it lawful copying?** Yes as copyright goes: lbp.govt.nz's
   copyright page (`e2428967…`) says its material, "This includes the
   Public Register", is Crown copyright and "may be used, copied and
   re-distributed free of charge in any format or media", with the source
   and copyright acknowledged. Decisions of the Board as a tribunal may in
   any case carry no copyright (Copyright Act 1994, s 27(1)); lawyer to say.
3. **What terms did we accept?** None beyond the copyright grant and the
   privacy statement (`7366101c…`); neither bars machine reading.
4. **What do privacy law and the statute limit?** For (b), the binding
   limit. Building Act 2004, s 299: the register's purpose is to let the
   public determine whether a person is licensed and their licensing
   history, choose a practitioner, know how to contact one, and "know which
   licensed building practitioners have been disciplined within the last 3
   years". **s 307: "A search of the register may be carried out only for a
   purpose referred to in section 299."** **s 308: a search for personal
   information not in accordance with ss 305 to 307 "constitutes an action
   that is an interference with the privacy of an individual under section
   69 of the Privacy Act 2020".** The register's own search page repeats
   it (`4c50071e…`). Copying the whole register into an estate that answers
   due-diligence questions about builders (use cases 6, 7, 12) is not a
   purpose s 299 names, and each search made to build it would be such an
   interference. So nothing was asked of the register until Arthur
   decided (ruling 132, 2 November 2026: copy); a lawyer should say whether a one-practitioner
   check made for a person choosing or checking that practitioner is within
   s 299(a)(i). The register also holds residential addresses, birth dates
   and contacts (s 301); the practitioner's page prints a postcode, an
   email and telephone numbers, which the estate keeps whole in
   `building_practitioners_private` (ruling 142, 4 November 2026).
   For (a), the Board publishes its decisions itself, naming the
   practitioner unless it directs otherwise; these are not searches of the
   register and s 307 does not reach them. The Privacy Act 2020 still asks
   fair use: the register itself shows discipline for three years
   (s 299(a)(iv), s 301(1)(l)(iii)). What the estate holds (rulings 142
   and 143, 4 November 2026): every decision captured stays on the
   published view, with `window_ends_on` (three years from its date),
   `past_window`, and `taken_down_on` once the Board's list no longer
   shows it. A name the Board withholds ("[OMITTED]") or a suppression
   direction in the item's summary blanks the name, LBP number and
   decision addresses on the lane's views, the row flagged `suppressed`,
   and the name, number and addresses as listed are kept whole in
   `building_practitioners_private`; each summary (it can name a property
   or a complainant) is kept whole there too. The region is the most the
   list publishes. Privacy, suppression and the window are applied at a
   product's export.
