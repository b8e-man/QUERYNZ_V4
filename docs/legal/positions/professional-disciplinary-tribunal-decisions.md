# Professional disciplinary tribunals' decisions: the Teachers Disciplinary Tribunal and the Health Practitioners Disciplinary Tribunal

hosts: teachingcouncil.nz, teachersdisciplinarytribunal.nz, hpdt.org.nz

Class: the published decisions of the New Zealand Teachers Disciplinary Tribunal (on the Teaching
Council's site, PDFs under `/assets/Files/PRDecisions/`) and of the Health Practitioners
Disciplinary Tribunal (`hpdt.org.nz`, whose pages and PDFs the MCP's courts corpus already partly
holds). The Lawyers and Conveyancers Disciplinary Tribunal and the Real Estate Agents Disciplinary
Tribunal publish through the Ministry of Justice's decisions finder and are covered by
`court-judgments.md`. Lane: `registers/courts` (ruling 140). Written 3 November 2026 by the
courts-sources survey before the hosts were asked, then checked against the pages it read
(`docs/research/courts-sources-2026-11-03.md`).

1. **Is the page open?** Partly, as read by the survey through the gate on 3 November 2026. The
   Teachers Disciplinary Tribunal's decisions now sit on its own site,
   `teachersdisciplinarytribunal.nz/disciplinary-tribunal-decisions` (a Squarespace page, open, no
   challenge; its `captchaSettings` are for donations only), with the PDFs on `res.cloudinary.com`, a
   shared file host: before any capture the position names that host scoped to the tribunal's own
   account path, never the whole host. The HPDT's search page is open, but its decisions-documents
   page sends a visitor to a login: that page is out (hard limit 6), and the MCP's 790 HPDT pages
   and PDFs stand. A login, challenge or block stops the host's capture; none is got around.
2. **Is storing it lawful copying?** Both are tribunals established by statute (Education and
   Training Act 2020; Health Practitioners Competence Assurance Act 2003), so their decisions are
   within the Copyright Act 1994, s 27(1)(g) (lawyer to confirm). Each decision is copied once, as
   published, unchanged.
3. **What terms did we accept?** None found before a decision; each site's terms page is read and
   landed at the first capture run, and a bar on robots found there stops that host.
4. **What does privacy law, or the statute, limit?** Both tribunals make non-publication orders
   (names of practitioners, complainants, patients, students) and publish redacted versions;
   decisions name the practitioner where no order is made, and the Teaching Council takes some
   decisions down after a period. The published version is captured and kept whole (ruling 142, 4
   November 2026); a redaction or order is a suppression flag beside the rows it reaches with the
   source's words, the names it covers kept in `courts_private` and never in a `pub.court_*` view
   (hard limit 16); a decision the publisher takes down is kept and flagged withdrawn with the date
   (never deleted, hard rule of the store); nothing is shown outside the local
   network (hard limit 13); no new connection about a named person without Arthur's word (hard
   limit 14).

**Crawl delay:** robots.txt read for a stated delay only. **Floor we use:** 5 seconds between
requests, one at a time, off-peak, each file fetched once.
