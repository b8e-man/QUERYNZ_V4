owns: schema `mcpsource` (`cursors`: per corpus, the MCP's records taken; `arrivals`: per record taken, its corpus, `seq`, id, url and the hash it landed as, `new` false for an unchanged re-arrival; `catch`: the catch log, a line per record not landed, with its stage, reason and the day a later run landed it; `drift`: each field or type the recorded shapes in `shapes.json` do not hold); the documents of source `query-nz` in `docs` (through the landing wrapper only): each record's JSON, keys sorted, no spaces, without `seq`, named `<corpus>/<id>` and by its `url`; a record served changed is a second document under the same names
reads: the ingest MCP `query-nz-ingest` (Arthur's provider, rulings 78, 94, 96) at http://127.0.0.1:8646/mcp through the tunnel unit `qv4-mcp-tunnel`: `corpora` and `records` only, never its `search` or `measure` in the pull; one request at a time, 200 records a page, a pause between pages, each call under a time limit, a slow or dropped call waited for and asked again (30 s doubling to 10 minutes, six tries)
publishes: nothing in `pub`; the documents are read by the lanes over them (`registers/beehive`; `registers/parliament`, `registers/ministers_diaries`, `registers/hansard` to come), each taking a record's newest version by `seq` from `mcpsource.arrivals`
outside check: each corpus's cursor against the MCP's own `ingest_cursor` from `corpora`, with every record taken either an arrival or a catch line (`python -m support.mcpsource` prints both; red when a corpus fails, a record is caught or a field is new to us); `tests/test_mcpsource.py::test_the_live_server_still_serves_the_recorded_shape` (slow) holds the live server to `shapes.json`
jobs: proposed, the integrator queues: `qv4-mcp-pull` (processor, 0.3 GB, nightly 02:30 NZDT, `python -m support.mcpsource`, deadline 4 h under its 24 h interval, query time limit 60 s, the call limit 180 s; a run that ends early on its budget resumes from its cursor next night); `qv4-mcp-rewalk` (processor, 0.3 GB, weekly Sunday 01:00 NZDT, `python -m support.mcpsource --rewalk`, deadline 8 h, query time limit 60 s: the MCP may change a record in place, its `updated_at` moving, where a cursor never looks; a changed record lands as a second document); `qv4-mcp-adopt` once, before the first nightly run: `python -m support.mcpsource --adopt scratch_mcp_1_pull2` carries mcp-1's first pull (the sample database) into the live `mcpsource` and lands its documents again from the store, no request to the MCP
